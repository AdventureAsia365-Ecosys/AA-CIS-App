# AA-1143 — One budget per connection: total plus per-app shares as rows, CIS takes the remainder

Tech lead decision G3 (S285, 19/11/2026): budgets become rows `(app, connection_key)`, edited on one screen. Lane l11
of S285 delivers the expand-first slice across CIS (migrations 455 / 456 + code), AA-Partner (the CRM copy, dual-read)
and AA-TripPlanner-Web (log-only read). The contract step (retire `crm.ai.connection_monthly_cap_usd`, the split API's
`cis_share_usd` / `crm_share_usd`, `cis.spend.connection_provider_budget_usd`) is a later lane.

## Decisions

- **One append-only table for totals and shares** (`shared.llm_connection_budget`, CIS 455): app NULL = the total,
  else an app of `shared.llm_app_profile` other than cis (FK + CHECK). Every change is a new version row (the Settings
  model: history = the table itself); `shared.llm_connection_budget_current` is the row in force. A removed share is a
  version with `budget_usd` NULL; a total is never removed.
- **CIS has no row: it takes the remainder.** A statement trigger (AFTER INSERT, transition table) refuses shares above
  the total and writes the remainder into `llm_provider_connection.monthly_budget_usd`. The gateway keeps enforcing that
  column through the cached registry: no new read on the call path, and the remainder follows a share change in the
  same statement. Not a DEFERRABLE constraint trigger: the migration preflight (X1) refuses trigger work after the
  role switch's post-check. A save is therefore one INSERT statement (decreases first, then the total, then increases).
- **CIS spend counts an app with its own share only above it** (`shared/connection_pool_spend.py`, the same rule
  as `catalog.v_llm_connection_cap_v2.cis_pool_spent_usd`): CIS + apps without a share count whole; TripPlanner, once
  it has a row, pays from its share and only its overflow eats the CIS part. Together: no app's spend is counted twice
  and the connection total holds whatever TripPlanner's switch says.
- **Seed (CIS 456) is read at apply time**: total = the provider budget setting value, else the CIS cap; CRM share =
  provider − CIS cap when provider > CIS cap. The CIS role cannot read the CRM setting (root ADR 0008a), and today the
  tech lead kept the three numbers in sum by hand (acc1 100 = 95 + 5), so provider − CIS is the CRM share. The file
  checks the result (CIS cap unchanged when provider ≥ CIS; lowered to the provider budget when lower or when CIS had
  none) and logs a NOTICE per connection. The page compares the CRM row with the CRM setting.
- **The CRM gets a copy, pushed by CIS** (the CRM app profile pattern, crm.ops.v1 `PUT /llm/connection-shares`, the
  staff member's own token): no CIS view is granted to `crm_app` and no grant is added. The copy names every
  connection with a total: the CRM's share, or 0 (a CRM without a share may not spend there: its ledger is not CIS's,
  so its spend would count nowhere). A lower share is sent before the CIS rows are written (a refusal writes nothing),
  a higher one after (a failed send is reported, "Send to CRM" retries); at no moment is the total exceeded.
- **TripPlanner** reads `catalog.v_llm_connection_cap_v2` (CIS 456, `SELECT` to `tripplanner` only, the control plane
  it already reads under ADR 0009 d13) and is log-only first: setting `tripplanner.spend.connection_share_enforce`
  (bool, default false, maker-checker) turns on leaving an over-share connection out under the dev spend rules.
- **The Connections drawer** shows the CIS cap read-only with the budget summary and a link to Budgets;
  `PATCH /admin/llm-connections/{key}` refuses a changed `monthly_budget_usd` on a connection with a total (422).
- **Tighten-only roles** (S279) may lower a cap, never raise one: no app's part, the CIS remainder included, may grow
  (so moving money from a share to CIS is an administrator's change).

## Changed

- The AA-1113 "Connection budget split" table on Budgets is replaced by "Connection budgets" (editable). Its API
  (`GET /admin/ai-gateway/connection-split`) stays until the contract step.
- 455 is classed contract, not expand: its re-runnable DDL (CREATE OR REPLACE, DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS, guard block) is
  outside the expand lint; it ships in the same migration-only PR as 456 anyway.

## Tradeoffs

- Writing the remainder into `monthly_budget_usd` (a derived copy) rather than reading the rows on the call path:
  zero call-path cost and no change to the gateway's cache, at the price of a column that must not be edited by hand
  (the PATCH refusal, the page's "CIS cap out of step" state).
- The CRM copy can be behind after a failed send; it is only ever behind on the safe side (a rise not yet sent).
- `catalog.v_llm_connection_cap_v1` (CIS 452) is left as it is (whole-ledger spend against the CIS cap); nothing
  enforces from it yet, v2 is the rule. Retire v1 with the contract step.
