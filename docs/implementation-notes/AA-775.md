# AA-775 — Fence the style guide as "never a source" + F17 `pace_promise`

Session S235, lane A-T9 (Claude Code). Stacked on AA-773 (PR #714, `fix/aa-773-offered-status`).

## Decisions
- **Fence text** = plan 09 §3.3 verbatim (from chị Thư `write.py:116-118`). It keeps the old
  "follow every rule in it, including each \"do not\"" clause, then adds "How this brand writes, in its own
  words. This is style and never a source: never cite it, never repeat one of its example sentences, and
  never state its claims about pace, rest, balance or difficulty as facts about this trip unless the
  content seed says so." The "unless the content seed says so" clause resolves Terra's own style-guide rule
  "Explain the pace clearly".
- **`pace_promise` is an F17_trip_fact finding, blog only.** `TripFactContext` gets `channel` and
  `brand_name`; `find_trip_fact_issues` adds `pace_claims(...)` only when `channel == "blog"`. LinkedIn (and
  any context built without a channel, e.g. older tests) never sees it, so a LinkedIn post never holds on it.
  No new gate name: F17 is already blocking, quoted (`_QUOTED_GATES`) and in `CHEAP_REPAIR_GATES`, so the
  finding goes to the Haiku sentence repair on `t9_repair`, and the attempt-3 `delete_flagged` cut is the
  deterministic fallback (S218 AA-736 lesson: a new blocking rule names its fix).
- **Regex** = plan §3 #3 plus `pacing` (`the pacing across days three to fourteen…` was a real r1 case and
  is the same claim). Body sentences only (prose, bullet, FAQ answer); title/meta are not checked.
- **Exclusions** (each one tested):
  1. the configured CTA (`_body_spans(text, cta)` — the same CTA protection every F17 check uses);
  2. **CTA paraphrases — decided YES (Batch 1 open question).** A sentence or clause (after `, ; : —`)
     that OPENS with an invitation verb (ask/speak/talk/chat/contact/reach out/get in touch/enquire/write/
     email/call) and names the brand (CTA brand word or first word of `brand_name`) or "us" within four
     words. Examples: "Talk to an Atlas & Hearth travel designer … your pace", "If this has your attention,
     talk to …", "Ask Terra Family Expeditions to plan … the pace". The brand's own line ("Terra Family
     Expeditions builds journeys … at a pace that …") opens with no invitation verb and IS flagged — that
     is exactly the style-guide paste the issue is about. Rationale: the CTA invites the reader to set their
     own pace; it promises nothing about this trip, and a repair there would fight `ensure_cta`.
  3. "the rest of …" (remainder) — only that occurrence is skipped; a second pace word in the same sentence
     still counts;
  4. seed-backed: the pace word's stem is in the moment's own source (`ctx.source` = own seed lines +
     Facts), or ≥60% of the content words in a ±6-word window are. Same thresholds as the Batch 1 replay.
- Message: `states a pace or rest promise the source does not make ('<word>') — cut it, or say only what the
  source says: '<sentence>'` — parses as one item with `sentence_repair._BRAND_QUOTE_RE` (no `: '` inside
  the problem part).

## Tradeoffs
- The fence also changes **T8** (angle prompt, `acp_angle_gate/prompts.py:122`) and **T2**
  (`content_generation/batch_prompt.py:124` and `flag_fix_node.py:217`, AA-792 already merged). Intended:
  the style guide is never a source anywhere. The T2 rerun measured the pre-fence text — known confound.
  No T2 test pins a hash of `render_brand_block` output (`test_aa792_t2_tenant_voice.py` pins only the
  brand_fit judge prompt); the A1 golden SHA test (`test_aa792_a1_prompt_golden.py`) does not use
  `render_brand_block` and stays green.
- Seed support is a word test, not meaning: if the seed says "rest" anywhere, every "rest" sentence passes
  (Trail Pulse "Rest before fatigue sets in" passes). Chosen to keep 0 false holds; misses are paid for by
  the fence in the prompt.
- The rule is wide on Terra blogs: in the S234 replay 22 of 32 blogs would get ≥1 finding → one Haiku
  sentence repair each (≈ +$0.002/blog). A finding that survives repair is cut at attempt 3, so the
  hold risk is low, but the r5 hold count must be read.
