## Decisions

- `status` registered, `registered_by` tech_lead, `registered_on` 2026-11-19, exactly the fields the `w5-t9`
  registration set. `sample_ids` unchanged: they are already the full non-trap GATE lists of each writer on 1.7.0
  (S1 less `s1-027`, over the judge's evidence cap), which `test_each_sample_is_every_non_trap_gate_item_of_its_writer`
  pins. `runner draft` would re-draw them by seeded hash and could not express the `s1-027` exclusion, so the file's
  list stays the sample of record (the draft step's contract is "a person saves it with status registered").
- The judge pin, decision (rule, margin, alpha), requirements, power block, metrics, targets and `cap_usd` are not
  touched.
- The notes gain one registration sentence each (who approved, the measured estimate and power). The S1 note's stale
  sentence "no a1 fire share, so the plan prices every claim as fired" now says the share was measured (C1: R1 fire
  6797de2f on the 25 GATE controls, 400 of 1669 claims, planning share 0.24). The note is part of the hashed document,
  so these edits are in the registered sha (the lock pins the file as registered, before any run).
- Lock: `registered.lock.json` gains `w5-s1` and `w5-t2` (sha256 of the canonical JSON, `prereg.sha256_of`); no other
  entry touched.

  the runner reads.
