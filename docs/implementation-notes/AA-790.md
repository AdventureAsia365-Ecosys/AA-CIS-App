Plan: root `.tmp-session/s250/plans/AA-790.json` (S250) with the S250 schedule §5 corrections and the merged GO-S251
memo (root `docs/audits/2026-10-24-S250-s251-go-no-go/README.md`, items 6a and 6b). Stacked on

## Decisions

- **D1 — the integrity check is a repair-contract invariant, not a gate (GO memo 6a).** Invariant 7
  `integrity_kept` lives in `services/quality/repair_contract.py` (`integrity_problems`, `enforce_step`) and detects
  nothing itself: it delegates to `hygiene.integrity_problems`, which now also reads the new
  `text_integrity.structure_findings`. So the sentence splice, the FAQ regeneration check, the first-line check, the
  admin edit path (`edit_findings`) and the new step guard all call one function. Nothing is added to the gate
  ledger or the quality registry, so the T10 gate count after AA-924 does not move and the 4-week
  gate freeze holds.
- **D2 — three new detectors in `text_integrity`, all paragraph-level (`span: None`).**
  `split_paragraphs` (r4 #18: a prose paragraph with no end mark followed by one that continues it — lower-case or
  And/But/Which/Because/…), `open_paragraphs` (a prose paragraph that stops with no end mark and is not continued —
  what is left when the final pass cuts the lower-case half of a split, or a trim drops the paragraph that finished
  the sentence) and `dangling_tails` (r4 #4/#12 shape: a sentence that ends on an article, conjunction or
  possessive). `span: None` keeps them out of `fix_fragments` (no new deletes) and out of F13_repeat / F13_hygiene
  (no new holds or warnings): they only drive reverts, new-vs-before, plus the census.
- **D3 — prepositions are not tail words.** English strands them ('a place worth coming back to.', 'what it is made
  of.', 'the people you travel with.'); the tail list is articles, conjunctions, 'than' and possessive
  determiners. An upper-case 'A' ('Plan A.') never fires.
- **D4 — `settle` for steps that run before the final pass.** `_StepGuard` compares `text_integrity.settle(before)`
  with `settle(after)` (the text `final_pass` would leave, without its residue read), so a step is not reverted for
  a duplicate or a lower-case fragment the final pass deletes anyway — a hazard caveat or a trip-fact correction is
  not lost for a defect that would never reach the gates. `length_trim.trim_to_band` runs after the final pass and
  is compared raw (`settle=False`). `final_pass` was refactored into `_fix_all` + residue so `settle` is the same
  code path, byte for byte (test).
- **D5 — what is wrapped.** `hygiene.autofix`, `hygiene.ensure_cta` (must keep: never reverted, its problems are
  logged as `kept …`), `trip_facts.add_family_caveats`, `drop_restated_faq`, `drop_unanswerable_faq`,
  `drop_subjectless_faq` (both calls), `real_first_faq`, `trip_facts.fix_trip_facts`, `length_trim.trim_to_band`.
  Not wrapped: `_enforce_faq_contract` and `_fix_first_line` (LLM steps with their own `integrity_problems` check)
  and `final_pass` (it is the fixer).
- **D6 — logs and degraded.** A reverted step's own log is replaced by its `reverted <step>: <problem>` lines (its
  edits are gone, so "removed 2 FAQ pairs" would be false; `faq_dropped` goes to 0). The lines go into the existing
  admin-only F13_autofix flag (and F7_faq_autofix for the FAQ droppers' own logs). Each revert is counted on the job
  and `_degraded_final` adds one soft `degraded: repair_contract` entry with detector `t9_step` (same name and
  severity as the AA-925 sentence-repair reverts, so the Content Quality degraded counts already show it). Soft =
  counted, never a hold. One `t9_step_contract_revert` warning per revert.

## Changed (vs the plan)

- Plan slice A put `guard_step` in `text_integrity.py`; per GO memo 6a and schedule §5 it is `repair_contract.
  enforce_step` + `service._StepGuard`, with the detectors in `text_integrity`.
- Plan step 2 said residue "stays visible as F13_repeat". Not done: surfacing the new detectors in F13 would change a
  gate's verdicts during the freeze, and the census found 0 hits on the 70 local stored pieces anyway. They are a
  census number (`scripts/aa790/integrity_census.py`) until the freeze ends.
- Added `open_paragraphs` (not in the plan): without it, the final pass turns a split paragraph into an unfinished
  one that no detector sees (found while writing the settle test).

## Tradeoffs

- **Cost of the guard.** On the longest golden blog (1,607 words) one `settle` is ~0.24 s and one
  `integrity_problems` ~0.19 s. A step that changes nothing is skipped (`after == before`), and a per-job memo keeps
  one settle per distinct text, so a typical attempt adds ~1–2 s of CPU on a blog, a fraction of that on LinkedIn.
  Pure Python on the worker, no LLM, $0.
- **A revert can cost a step its fix.** If a trim leaves a broken paragraph, the over-long blog is not trimmed and
  the length gate routes it as before AA-984; if a FAQ drop is reverted, the non-answer stays for F16/F7 to report.
  Broken text reaching the tenant is worse than either.
- **`drop_subjectless_faq` reverted:** its `subject_short` count is kept from the dropped run, so the FAQ
  regeneration may skip `fix_subjects` when every failing answer had been dropped. Rare (the drop has to break the
  text first); left as is.
