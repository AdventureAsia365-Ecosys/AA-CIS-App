# AA-786 — F9_cta_fact v1.1

Lane A-T9, session S235. Branch `fix/aa-786-f9-v11`.

## Decisions

- **Gate-1 policy (S235 defaults, Nghiệp to confirm):**
  1. A non-pass F9 verdict with no checkable reason (no recognised code, notes only, and no
     sentence of the piece quoted in the notes) is a **warn** (`F9_cta_fact`, `blocking: false`),
     never a hold. Same precedent as AA-770 "an outage is not a verdict".
  2. On **LinkedIn**, an F9 product-truth failure (unsupported claim / TEXT_INTEGRITY) left after
     the **last** attempt is **cut** (`sentence_repair.delete_flagged`). A CTA_NOT_REFLECTED F9
     failure is never cut and still holds (`hygiene.ensure_cta` restores the CTA verbatim, so it is
     rare). *Superseded in part by the PR #709 review — see the orchestrator decisions below.*
- **Orchestrator decisions, PR #709 review (under AA-770: `held` is an admin-only label; the tenant
  always receives the content):**
  - **P1** — the F9 entry is waived (`blocking: false, waived: true`) ONLY when every flagged truth
    sentence was actually cut. If any is left (the hook or CTA the cut refuses, a cut that would
    orphan a pronoun, a quote that matches no sentence), F9 stays blocking and the piece is held for
    the admin; the safe sentences are still cut. `sentences_cut` / `sentences_left` are recorded on
    the F9 ledger entry and on the repair_log entry in every case (`cut_reason` when not waived,
    `waived_reason` when waived).
  - **P2** — `_cut_truth_items` runs only on the last attempt (the last-try stop, and the attempt-3
    keep-previous stop), never when an earlier attempt stops on a non-repairable gate.
  - **N1** — after a cut: the writer summary is cleared (it may state the cut claim; a None summary
    is a normal soft-fail outcome, AA-498), the embedding is recomputed on the cut text (one embedding
    call; the old one is kept if it fails), and F4_extreme_length / F14_social_shape are re-run on the
    cut text and replace their ledger entries. A cut that pushes the post under its word band (it was
    not under before) or to an empty text is not waived: F9 stays blocking with that reason.
  - **N2** — cut counting compares tag-stripped, whitespace-folded text on both sides.
  - **B1** — a claim is attributed to the title/meta only when it is not in the body at all
    (`locate_sentence` finds no sentence AND its folded words are not a substring of the folded
    body); `_seo_field_of` needs ≥ 4 words (the `locate_sentence` floor).
- Tag strip happens in `run_quality_gates` (the F9 call gets `strip_citation_tags(content_text)`)
  and again at the top of `gate_brand_voice` (defence in depth). F1/F5 keep the tagged text.
- TEXT_INTEGRITY gets its own contract field `broken_sentences: [{sentence, problem}]` instead of
  sharing `flagged_phrases` with the style and fact codes — `flagged_phrases` is one flat list for
  every code, so a quote there cannot be attributed to TEXT_INTEGRITY. A broken sentence counts
  only when the judge also returns the `TEXT_INTEGRITY` code and the sentence is locatable in the
  piece (`sentence_repair.locate_sentence`); otherwise it is dropped like an unjustified fact flag.
- Violation shape for TEXT_INTEGRITY: `TEXT_INTEGRITY: '<sentence>'` (one per sentence), the same
  `<problem>: '<sentence>'` shape `_BRAND_QUOTE_RE` already parses for F2/F11/F12/F13/F15/F17/F18.
- The notes-only fallback looks for a quoted string ('…', "…", “…”) of ≥ 4 words in `notes` that
  `locate_sentence` finds in the piece; the located sentence becomes a TEXT_INTEGRITY violation.
- Title/meta: `gate_brand_voice(seo_title=, meta_description=)`; passed only for `channel == "blog"`
  (the only channel with a search title since S234 round 4). The judge sees a
  `SEARCH TITLE` / `META DESCRIPTION` block inside PIECE. An unsupported claim whose sentence is the
  title or the meta becomes a `title unsupported claim: …` / `meta unsupported claim: …` violation,
  separate from the body claims; F9 is blocking only when a body violation remains (same
  `body_blocking` rule F17/F18 use).
- LinkedIn cut bookkeeping: the F9 entry keeps its violations unchanged (the admin sentence actions
  parse them); repair_log gets one entry with `repair_mode` `f9_truth_cut` (`sentences_cut` ≥ 1) or
  `f9_cut_refused` (nothing cut). The cut runs before `_hold_or_ship`, which is unchanged (no
  `channel` argument — `hold_failure` already skips a non-blocking entry).
- `_log_t10_judge_call` extra now carries `prompt_version: "f9_v1.1"`, `piece_tag_free` and `reasonless`, so the
  live check can read the t10_judge row in `shared.llm_call_log.quality_signal`.

## Changed

  `TEXT_INTEGRITY`; new contract field `broken_sentences`; notes-only fallback replaced (quote →
  TEXT_INTEGRITY, no quote → warn); title/meta claims split from body claims; blocking computed.

- `service._cut_truth_items` (+ `_after_cut`): the LinkedIn cut at the last-attempt stops (attempt-3
  keep-previous branch and the last-try branch). `_hold_or_ship` is unchanged.
- `sentence_repair.truth_items` returns `(items, unlocated)`; new `truth_quotes`.
- Prompt for the F9 judge changed → no SHA-pinned prompt is affected (the SHA guards in
  `test_aa771_s234_voice_contract.py` pin the writer SYSTEM_PROMPT and the single-line prompt, not
  the judge), so they are untouched.

## Tradeoffs

- The LinkedIn cut re-runs only the cheap length gates (F4, F14) on the cut text — no extra judge
  call; the stored text still goes through `_final_integrity` (final_pass + F13_repeat residue)
  after it. The other ledger entries describe the pre-cut text — the only change is removed
  sentences.
- Title/meta F9 claims are not yet fed back to `_fit_seo_meta` (follow-up, as the plan allowed);
  they ship as a warn for the admin.
- `_cut_truth_items` is a small separate helper so AA-777 item 2 can extend it to blogs without
  rewriting it; the blog path is untouched here.
