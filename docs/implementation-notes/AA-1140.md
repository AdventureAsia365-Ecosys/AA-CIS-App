# AA-1140 — Ecosystem status page (backend)

The tech lead approved the "Ecosystem status" page on 19/11 (S285 advisory, mockup v2). It replaces the content of
`/admin/overview` with one live and period status page for CIS, TripPlanner, Booking and the partner CRM. This note
covers the migration (CIS 475) and the backend (`GET /admin/overview/status`, the sampler job). The page itself has its
own note, `AA-1140-ui.md`.

## Decisions

- **Tables in `acp_shared`, not `shared`.** The issue says `shared.ops_sample` / `shared.ops_event`. Data standard
  DM-1.5 forbids new CIS-private tables in `shared`, and only the CIS status page reads them. They are
  `acp_shared.ops_sample` and `acp_shared.ops_event` (CIS 475), next to the staff audit log and the docs feedback.
- **The sampler is a `shared.job` kind queued by the api's ops metrics sampler.** `ops_status_sample` runs once per
  5-minute UTC bucket (idempotency key `ops_status_sample:<bucket>`), the same pattern as the route watch and the
  usage rollup. It is background, deploy-gate exempt and has `max_attempts` 1: the next bucket is the retry. A run
  that starts more than 10 minutes after its bucket writes nothing (`skipped_late`), so a sample never carries the
  wrong time. Switch `OPS_STATUS_SAMPLER` (default on). It runs on the worker, which uses the same task role as the
  api, so the reads need no new IAM.
- **What the sampler reads, all free.** ECS DescribeServices of the seven services (CIS api, worker, admin; Booking
  api, admin; CRM api, web), DescribeTaskDefinition once per new revision (cached in the process), CloudWatch
  DescribeAlarms grouped by App tag (the overview's own code), the public health and readiness checks of CIS,
  Booking and the CRM, the `shared.job` backlog (the jobs_starved rule), the busiest DB role, the XID age and the
  sessions against `max_connections`.
- **Events.** Each ECS deployment becomes a `deploy` event at its creation time (dedupe `deploy:<deployment id>`),
  closed when its rollout ends. A failed rollout is a SEV2 `rollout_failed` event, closed once a later rollout of
  the same service completed (a rollback or a new deploy) or ECS no longer lists the failed deployment (review fix:
  it stayed open for good before). A firing alarm is an `alarm` event
  from its state change time, closed on the first tick that no longer sees it firing. A service below its desired
  count is a `service_down` event (SEV1 when nothing runs) until it is back. These give the charts their deploy
  markers and alarm bands.
- **Golden signals are read when the page asks, not sampled.** CloudWatch GetMetricData in the step of the chosen
  range, cached in Redis (never without Redis). The S285 advisory priced continuous sampling at about 3.50 USD a
  month, a read on page open at well under 1 USD. About 25 metrics a read (0.00025 USD; GetMetricData has no free
  tier).
- **The cache key does not move with each refresh (review fix).** A live preset ends at the next step, so the 1 h
  view (60 s steps) moved its key every minute and every 60 s page refresh was a paid miss (about 9-11 USD a month
  for one tab left open). `fetch_range` reads the current period on a grid of max(step, 5 minutes): its end is
  rounded up to that grid (the same span), and `relay` lays the series back on the page's steps. The previous period
  (compare) is complete: it is read on an hourly grid, widened to cover the page's range, and cached 1 hour. So a
  view left open costs at most one read per 5 minutes per preset: about 288 reads, 0.07 USD a day (about 2 USD a
  month), shared by every viewer of the same preset. A custom range is fixed and read as is.
- **Steps follow the span** (mockup v2): up to 90 minutes 1-minute steps, up to 8 hours 5 minutes, up to 3 days
  hourly, longer 6-hourly. Presets end at the next step boundary so viewers in the same step share one cache entry.
- **One endpoint, sections with their own `as_of`.** `GET /admin/overview/status?range=&from=&to=&compare=` returns
  `verdict`, `thresholds`, `sections` (signals, apps, runtimes, resources, jobs, ai, cost, events, delivery) and
  `sources`. A failing section becomes `unavailable` with a reason. A signal with no source yet is `not_measured`;
  the verdict lists failed sources under `unknown` and never counts them as healthy.
- **"All systems normal" needs every core source (review fix).** The verdict is `ok` only when the CloudWatch
  signals, the app tiles, a fresh runtimes sample (at least one ECS service sampled within the freshness window),
  jobs, resources and AI all answered. With nothing open and a core source missing it is `partial` (neutral
  headline naming the missing sources); with neither the signals nor the runtimes it is `unknown`. Open items still
  give `attention`. Before, the green headline showed right after deploy (no sample yet) or with CloudWatch refused.
- **One freshness setting for latest samples (review fix).** The runtimes grid, the health tiles and the resources
  tiles (ECS tasks, busiest DB role) all treat a sample older than `cis.ops.status_stale_factor` x 5 minutes as not
  current (`store.fresh_since`); the resources section had a fixed 30-minute window.
- **Verdict severity.** SEV1: a runtime down, a failing health endpoint, no live worker. SEV2: a critical limit, a
  page-severity alarm, a failed rollout, a failed nightly probe. SEV3: a warn limit, a ticket alarm, an ops check
  bell, a guard that did not run, a red main branch. An ops check bell that was read counts as acknowledged.
- **Uptime.** Each runtime's share of 5-minute samples in which it was healthy (ok or warn = up, degraded or down =
  down, unknown = not measured), against `cis.ops.status_uptime_slo_pct`, with the error budget left.
- **Thresholds are settings** (CIS 475, area `ops`, maker-checker, guides in EN and VI): 21 keys
  `cis.ops.status_*` with the S285 advisory defaults. Two warn values reuse the CIS 469 in-app check keys
  (`cis.ops.role_connections_pct`, `cis.ops.xid_age_limit`), so a tile and its alert never disagree. A critical
  value on the wrong side of its warn value falls back to the warn value.

## Changed (against the issue)

- `booking.job`: there is no owner view or endpoint CIS may read (Booking's `/booking/ops/deploy-gate` accepts only
  the deploy workflow's OIDC token). The queue shows "not measured yet". Follow-up: a Booking job summary on the ops
  reader route, or a `bk-` count view granted to `cis_app` (a cross-app contract, risk review).
- Partner CRM traffic (requests, errors, p95): its API sits behind the shared load balancer. The target-group
  dimension cannot be looked up without `elasticloadbalancing:DescribeTargetGroups`, which the CIS role does not have.
  The tile says "not measured yet". Its health check, alarms, jobs (crm.ops.v1) and AI spend are on the page.
- TripPlanner Lambda versions and the Vercel web: no read on the CIS role. They show as "not measured yet" rows;
  their traffic and errors are in the charts.
- CRM activity line: no count CIS may read yet ("not measured yet").
- Nightly guards: the two probes CIS reads (tour liveness, freshness) come from `shared.kpi_daily`; the guards that
  run only on GitHub or print no verdict line are listed as "not measured yet" (`services/reports/probes.NOT_VISIBLE`).

## Tradeoffs

- 288 `ops_status_sample` jobs a day in `shared.job` (like the route watch's 96). This gives retries, the Jobs page
  view and the worker's resources, at the price of rows on the Jobs page. The Infra job-idle gate does not exempt the
  kind; a run takes a few seconds, so the 3 x 20 s idle wait sees it idle.
- Retention: 14 days for samples and 30 days for events are proposed in DM-7.1. There is no deletion job yet, so
  nothing is deleted until the tech lead approves the windows. About 4,000 sample rows a day.
- The DescribeTaskDefinition cache lives in the worker process. A new worker reads each revision once.

## Fix after the S289 live check (page only)

Four findings of the live check on Dev, fixed in the page; no API change.

- **F1, latency headroom.** The headroom line took the peak of every shown series against the API p95 limit, so the
  TripPlanner Lambda (own limit `lambda_p95_limit_ms`, 6 s) read "Headroom −668 ms" against 1.5 s.
  `tightestHeadroom()` (lib/opsStatus.ts) now takes each series against its own limit (`ChartSpec.limitFor`; latency:
  TripPlanner → the Lambda limit, the others → `p95_ms`), skips a series without one, and the line shows the tightest
  series with its name. A chart without any limit (requests per minute) keeps "Headroom: no value".
- **F2, quick chip clicks.** Each click built the next list from the view of the last render, and `router.replace`
  only updates the URL after its navigation, so the second of two quick clicks started from the old URL. Now every
  write is `nextViewQuery(location.search, change)` through the native `history.replaceState` (Next.js syncs it into
  `useSearchParams`, no server round trip), and chip rows, legend boxes and table sorts pass a function of the current
  view (`SetView` accepts `Partial<View> | (current) => Partial<View>`). Test: two clicks without a re-render keep both.
  The kit's `useUrlFilters` (core) was not changed; the page only reads through it.
- **F3, TripPlanner health chip.** The sampler checks the health of CIS, Booking and the partner CRM only, so
  TripPlanner's `health` is null. The chip now says why (EN + VI); another app with no sample says "no sample yet".
- **F4, app tile metrics at 1440.** The four metrics were a fixed 4-column grid in a ~250 px tile. They are now two
  pairs: four across in a wide tile (390 phone), two by two in a narrow one (the 4-up grid at 1440), never 3 + 1;
  labels stay on one line (ellipsis + title), values never wrap; spacing and radius from kit `SPACE` / `RADIUS`.

Should know: not checked in a browser locally (the admin proxy verifies a real staff token); check the four views on
the Vercel preview or on Dev after deploy (1440 / 390, the latency headroom text, two quick chip clicks).
