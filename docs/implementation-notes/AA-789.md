# AA-789 — T10 gate recall tuning (Report 12 §1.3)

S257 (29/10/2026). The S234 panel (rounds r2–r4) found defect classes the deterministic T10 gates did not catch
(root `docs/audits/2026-10-13-S234-t-series-rounds/12-gates-t2-tenants.md` §1.3, with the examples in reports 03,
04 and 07). This change widens the patterns behind existing gates only. No gate changes severity and no new gate is
added (standing rule since S246: no new blocking gates for 4 weeks).

## Decisions
- **F15_voice, contrast frames** (`hygiene.CONTRAST_FRAME_RES`, indices 8–11 appended, so earlier indices keep their
  meaning; `quality_gates._CONTRAST_RANK` ranks them): the reversed clause-final `Y, not X.`; a verb negation answered
  after `;` / `:` / a dash (`does not … ; it …`); a sentence-initial `Not …` fragment (capital N only); `less X, more Y`.
  The density rule (more than 1 frame per 150 words) still decides whether a piece gets a finding.
- **F15_voice, a frame inside a stock-quoted sentence** (`_in_stock_sentence`) no longer counts toward the density and
  gets no second finding: the stock repair rewrites that sentence anyway, and a second overlapping finding widened the
  sentence-repair span (existing test `test_not_as_pair_is_one_quoted_span`).
- **F15 stock lines in blogs**: `channel_spec.CROSS_CHANNEL_STOCK_PATTERNS` ('does not perform', 'tolerates them') is
  read by `hygiene.TEMPLATE_LINE_RES` on every channel. The panel found exactly these two in blogs (r4 #15, #17).
  'not a backdrop' and 'isn't an inconvenience' were LinkedIn-only findings and stay in `STOCK_PATTERNS` only.
- **F17_trip_fact**:
  - Hedge scope per list item (`_item_hedged`): a hedge covers its own item and the items after it, never an earlier
    one, except a collective predicate in the last item ('… are all optional'). r4 #14 put the boat between 'on foot'
    and 'optionally on horseback'; the old whole-clause hedge let it through.
  - Travelling by the vehicle an optional activity names counts as mentioning it (`_MODE_RE`: 'by boat').
  - Reverse flip (`included_as_optional`): a clause that calls a fully planned moment optional (r4 #18). A moment
    with any optional part is left to the forward check, and 'optional <noun>' names the noun, not the moment
    ('for optional trekking'), unless the noun is a moment word (day, visit, excursion …).
  - Time idioms (`_TIME_IDIOM_RE`): 'worn out by midday' (r4 #11 false positive), 'at the end of the day' … are not
    schedules.
  - Difficulty promises join `PACE_RE`: 'technical' before a route word, 'strenuous', 'gruelling', 'effortless'
    (r4 #14 'not a technical climb'). Blog-only like the rest of the pace check (AA-775).
  - `near_as_at`: a meal / stop the source puts NEAR a place, written AT / IN it ('lunch at Mörön', 7 r4 blogs).
- **F18_rarity**: 'unlike almost anything', 'anything else on earth', 'most of the world', 'the world has never',
  'significant fraction / share / proportion', 'hard to overstate'. A superlative with no scope ('one of the most
  coherent …') is backed only by the same superlative word in the source, no longer by any superlative there.
- **F13_leak**: the seed's naming-note vocabulary in `LEAK_RE` ('phonetic approximation', 'correctly spelled',
  'should be referred to as', '<name> best avoided'); `BRIEF_WORDING_RE` for the brief's 'one X, one Y, one Z' recipe
  and the angle's 'two named rivers'. `BRIEF_WORDING_RE` is read by `find_issues` only (sentence repair), never by
  autofix, because such a sentence can carry a fact.
- **F13_repeat**: `subjectless_section_openers` (a section opening on 'Access requires …' with no complement, r4 #16)
  and `paraphrased_repeats` (the same two-word descriptor ending in an adjective suffix, a different head noun, not the
  seed's own wording, plus a shared word or a pronoun opener: r4 #1 'living religious seat / institution'). Both are
  reported, never deleted by autofix.
- **F16 (FAQ dropper)**: `faq_answer_problem` adds `off_attribute` for weather, purpose ('use … for') and when
  questions whose first two answer sentences carry no word of that attribute (r4 #17, #19). F16 itself is unchanged:
  `drop_subjectless_faq` drops such pairs above the 3-pair floor and the FAQ regeneration answers them.

## Changed
- The issue asked for "F17 reverse flips + idioms"; pace / difficulty words and 'lunch at vs near' (same §1.3 row, r4
  fix 2) are in too.
- 'Digits vs words' and 'hashtag near-miss' (§1.3, "new checks") are out of scope here: they were listed as new checks,
  not recall tuning.

## Tradeoffs
- Narrow patterns over wide ones: each pattern was replayed on the 40 S234 pieces the panel scored, and every
  pattern that fired where the panel had not raised that defect was tightened (the paraphrase suffix list lost
  -ary/-ent/-ant/-ed/-ing after 'ecological boundary', 'settlement', 'hundred' false hits; the reversed frame skips
  prepositions after 'not'; 'optional trekking' no longer reads as the moment being optional).
- More F15 / F13 findings mean more cheap sentence repairs on attempt 1 (about $0.012 each, Report 12 §1.1). F15 still
  blocks on attempt 1 only and never holds; F13_repeat and F17/F18 keep their existing severity.
