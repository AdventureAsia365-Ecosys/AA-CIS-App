# AA-789 — T10 gate recall tuning (Report 12 §1.3)

S257 (29/10/2026). The S234 panel (rounds r2–r4) found defect classes the deterministic T10 gates did not catch
(root `docs/audits/2026-10-13-S234-t-series-rounds/12-gates-t2-tenants.md` §1.3). §1.3 is a table of classes with
no sentences; the examples come from the round reports 03 (r2), 04 (r3) and 07 (r4). This change touches existing
gates only. No severity flag changes and no new gate is added (standing rule since S246: no new blocking gates for 4
weeks).

## History (same session)
- Revision 1 widened patterns behind F13_leak, F13_repeat, F15_voice, F16, F17_trip_fact and F18_rarity.
- Revisions 2 and 3 narrowed them after two independent verifier rounds found clean travel sentences they flagged.
- A third verifier round (40 clean sentences with their own seeds) still found false positives under the gates that
  block AND can hold a tenant piece: F17_trip_fact ('by boat', per-item hedges, 'near' vs 'at'), F18_rarity
  ('significant share', 'the world has yet to', the scopeless-superlative rule), F13_leak ('one X, one Y, one Z',
  naming-note terms) and F13_repeat (paraphrased predicates). Each fix found a new false positive. That is regex
  whack-a-mole, and the tech lead's standing direction is precision over recall for blocking gates.
- **Revision 4 (this one)** keeps a widened pattern only when it is narrow, literal, and fired on none of the clean
  sentences of any verifier round. Everything else is reverted to `main`, and its panel examples are pinned in the
  tests as known misses.

## Decisions
- **Kept rule.** A widened pattern stays only if (a) it is literal (fixed words from a panel quote, not a sentence
  shape), (b) it fired on none of the clean sentences of the three verifier rounds, and (c) for F15, it cannot hold
  a piece.
- **Kept, F18_rarity** (`trip_facts.AA789_RARITY`, part of `RARITY_CLAIM_RE`; kept as its own constant so the tests
  can run it alone):
  - 'unlike almost anything / anywhere / any other' (r4 #16 'unlike almost anything else on earth'). `main` already
    had 'unlike anything'. Only 'almost' is added: 'Unlike most ger camps on the route, …' is copy.
  - 'anything else on earth / the planet' (r4 #16). 'anything' needs 'else'.
  - '(most of | the rest of) the world has / have never / rarely <verb>' (r4 #17 'most of the world has never
    considered', r3 #17 'most of the world has never seen'). Not 'the world has yet to settle on one' (round 3), not
    'barely', not a bare 'most of the world' ('Travellers from most of the world can get a visa').
- **Kept, F15_voice** (blocks on attempt 1 for one sentence repair, never holds: `quality_gates.never_holds`,
  `STYLE_ONLY_GATES`):
  - Contrast frame 8 (`hygiene.CONTRAST_FRAME_RES[8]`), the clause-final ', not X.' with a **closed list** of X:
    'ceremonial sentiment' (r4 #8), 'scenic preference' (r4 class 4) and '<word> frozen in time'. Revision 3's shape
    rule (an '-al / -ic / -ive / -ous / -ful' adjective on an abstract noun) and its stock nouns ('museum',
    'formality', 'postcard' …) are gone: round 3 flagged 'paid to the government, not a personal commission', 'a
    rescue centre …, not commercial entertainment', 'the check is strict, not a formality' and three more.
  - Contrast frame 9, the broetry pair 'Not X in. Y in.' (r3 #15 'Not squeezed in. Planned in.'): the second
    fragment must end on the same word as the first. It fired on no clean sentence.
  - The density rule still decides whether a piece gets a finding (more than 1 frame per 150 words).
  - `quality_gates._in_stock_sentence`: a frame inside a sentence a stock finding already quotes is not counted twice.
  - Blog stock lines (`channel_spec.CROSS_CHANNEL_STOCK_PATTERNS`, read by `hygiene.TEMPLATE_LINE_RES` on every
    channel):
    - 'does not perform for visitors / tourists / the camera', only at the clause end and only in a sentence with no
      time or season word (`_SCHEDULE_WORDS`: 'In winter the troupe does not perform for visitors.' is a schedule). A
      bare 'does not perform.' is no longer read: round 3 'Unlike at the old trekking camps, the herd at this
      sanctuary does not perform.' is an animal-welfare fact, and '… outside the festival days the troupe does not
      perform.' is a schedule.
    - 'tolerates them', only at the sentence end. Not 'tolerates you / us': round 3 '… an adult animal tolerates you
      only when you approach slowly.' is advice.
- **Kept, F17_trip_fact: time idioms** (`_TIME_IDIOM_RE`). 'worn out by midday' (r4 #11 false positive), 'at the
  end of the day' and similar phrases are not schedules. This change only removes findings.

## Changed (reverted to `main` in revision 4; each panel example is pinned in the tests as a known miss)
- F17: per-item hedge scope (`_item_hedged`, `_COLLECTIVE_HEDGE_RE`). Known miss r4 #14 'on foot, and by boat, and
  optionally on horseback'.
- F17: travelling 'by boat' as the boat ride (`_MODE_RE`, traveller and plan-statement context). Known miss r4 #5.
  Round 3 false positives: 'Families explore the shore on foot or by boat, depending on the weather …', 'On the lake
  day, families walk the shoreline …, and anyone who wants to can take a boat out', and a LinkedIn 'Boat rides and
  horseback riding are optional on day five, so each family picks …'.
- F17: the reverse flip `included_as_optional` (a planned moment called optional). Known miss r4 #18.
- F17: `near_as_at` ('lunch at X' when the source says near). Known misses r4 fix 2 and r3 'A stop in Mörön'. Round 3
  false positives: 'Long-distance buses from Ulaanbaatar to Khatgal make a stop in Mörön …', 'Lunch on day two is
  served on board, and the last stop is in Yen Duc village …'.
- F17: `PACE_RE` 'not a technical climb'. Known miss r4 #14.
- F18: 'significant / considerable share / fraction of the world'. Known miss r4 #19. Round 3 false positive: 'Lake
  Baikal holds about 20 percent of the world's unfrozen fresh surface water, a significant share …' (seed-backed).
- F18: 'the world has never / yet to'. Round 3 false positive: '… the world has yet to settle on one …'.
- F18: the scopeless superlative backed only by the same superlative word in the source. Known miss r4 #16 'one of
  the most coherent'. Round 3 false positive: 'The Tsaatan are among the most distinctive communities …' with the
  seed's 'one of Mongolia's most distinct'.
- F13_leak: `BRIEF_WORDING_RE` ('one X, one Y, one Z'). Known miss r2 #19. Round 3 false positive: the LinkedIn line
  'One driver, one guide, one pick-up point.'.
- F13_leak: naming-note terms ('phonetic approximation', 'correctly spelled'). Known miss r3 #14. Round 3 false
  positives: an FAQ pronunciation answer ('… Muren, which is only a phonetic approximation …') and 'Check that Mörön
  is correctly spelled on your domestic flight ticket …'.
- F13_repeat: `paraphrased_repeats`. Known miss r4 #1. Round 3 false positives: two '## Day N / The drive is a short
  scenic …' sections and 'The trail is a gentle gradual ascent … / … a gentle gradual descent …'.
- Already dropped in revisions 2–3 and still out: 'does not …; it …', 'less X, more Y', a lone 'Not …' fragment, 'two
  named rivers' (r3 #7), 'loose / common approximation', 'best avoided', a section opener on a bare relational noun
  (r4 #16), F16 `off_attribute`, 'strenuous' / 'gruelling' / 'effortless', and 'hard to overstate' (r4 #4).
- 'Digits vs words' and 'hashtag near-miss' (§1.3 "new checks") are out of scope: they are new checks, not recall
  tuning.

## Tradeoffs
- **Recall gained is small.** On the 40 stored S234 pieces, 2 pieces gain a finding (`main` → branch), and both
  are classes the panel raised (see Should know). Most §1.3 examples stay misses: 'by boat', the reverse flip, near
  vs at, 'technical', 'significant fraction', the scopeless superlative, the brief recipe, the naming note and the
  paraphrase repeat. Telling those apart from clean copy needs meaning: does the copy contradict what the seed plans?
  That is the planned post-repair integrity check (Report 12 §1.4, an LLM reading the seed and the final piece), not
  more regex.
- Two near-misses of revision 3's tests fire on `main` through older checks: 'Boat rides and horse riding cost
  extra.' (`optional_as_fixed`'s hedge list has no 'cost extra') and the reviewer's two-lodge pair (`near_duplicates`).
  They are pinned as unchanged `main` behaviour. Fixing them is a precision change for its own issue, not part of
  this one.
- The kept F15 frames can still fire on a plain contrast that uses the exact closed-list words. That is unlikely,
  and F15 never holds a piece: at worst it costs one sentence repair on attempt 1 (about $0.012, Report 12 §1.1).

## Should know
- **Severity in effect.** No `*_BLOCKING` flag changed. Under F18_rarity, which blocks and can hold, the only additions
  are the three literal `AA789_RARITY` phrases. Under F13_leak, F13_repeat and F17_trip_fact nothing new can fire.
  The time idioms only remove findings.
- **Evidence, all $0, no DB, no LLM:**
  - Round-3 harness (40 clean sentences with seeds; every gate: F15 violations, frames, template lines, F17, F18, all
    F13 buckets) on `origin/main` vs this branch: **0 new findings on any gate**, including F15. Output identical.
  - Offline replay (`scripts/aa789_recall_replay.py run` on the decoded AA-783 export, `origin/main` vs branch, then
    `compare`): 2 of 40 pieces gain findings. n15 (blog, panel heavy) gains F18 on 'unlike almost anything' (r4
    #16). n34 (LinkedIn, panel light) gains an F15 contrast-frame finding on 'not ceremonial sentiment' and 'not a
    scenic preference'. These are the two frames the panel cited for that piece (r4 #8, 'deciding_defects' in the
    panel key). They need one sentence repair on attempt 1 and never hold. No piece gains an F13 or F17 finding.
  - Unit tests: every clean sentence of the three verifier rounds is a negative test. The 40 round-3 sentences run
    against each kept pattern alone, and through the gates against the messages the reverted patterns produced.
- The export holds real tenant copy: keep it in the gitignored `.tmp-session/`, never in this repo.
- Test fixtures are marked "quote" (the panel's words, all shorter than 8 words) or "synthetic, shape of …". The
  round-3 sentences and seeds are the verifier's synthetic text, not stored copy (AA-919 golden-text guard: 0
  findings).
