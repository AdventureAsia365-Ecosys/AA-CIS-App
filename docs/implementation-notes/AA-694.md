# AA-694 — A3 question landing: transit, country scope, landing belongs

Design: `docs/architecture/at-series-v2-design.md` §4.2 (A3-5) and §8 (Q3, Q4, Q5, Q9).

## Part 1 — transit rule (S204): already in place
`precompute_question_landings()` skips Segments that `classify_exclusion()` marks transit (856 of 3,362 on Dev), and a question lands only on its own Segment's atoms. Remaining: 192 atoms typed `transit` by atomize that the rule misses (127 Segments, 564 of 9,471 counted questions, 6%). That is the `a3_activity_type` question (rule vs atomize disagreement), not a blind rule change.

## Part 2 — country scope (S204)
- **Two Jev Questions** (migration 185, shadow), with wording ported from Ms. Thư's ticket 04:
  - `a3_question_foreign` (loose rule);
  - `a3_question_about_here` (strict rule; generic questions are out, Q3).

  Both are asked in one call about the state `{question, countries}`.
- **Scope = the Segment's own tour countries** (Q4), joined from `raw_tours.country`. The countries are in the state and the subject key, not in the wording, so the wording hash stays fixed. A Segment that gains a country is asked again. A Segment with no known country is not filtered.
- **Where:** `filter_candidates_by_country_scope()` runs in `precompute_question_landings()` after the candidate shortlist and before embedding and landing, so a dropped question is never embedded or counted. One Verdict per distinct (country set, question), 4 at a time; the cache makes repeats free.
- **Drop rule:** `foreign` is a confident yes, or `about_here` is a confident no, and only in enforce mode (ADR 0007). In shadow the Verdicts are logged as calibration data.
