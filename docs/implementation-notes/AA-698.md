# AA-698 — A1 judge gates on brand fit, not cross-brand distinctiveness

## Decisions
- **A1 (not `is_tenant_rewrite`): `judge_score = brand_fit_score`**, still capped at 6.0 when the
  mission is absent. `cross_brand_distinct` is still asked, logged and persisted, but does not gate.
  T2 (tenant brands) keeps `min(brand_fit, cross_brand_distinct)` unchanged.
- Why: the master rewrite is brand-neutral by design (AA-535, CONTEXT.md A1). "How clearly would
  this differ if rewritten for another brand" is T2's job. GPT-4.1 was lenient on that axis;
  GPT-5.6 Luna (s1_judge route since S200/AA-659) scores it literally.
- No new stage/route, no ADR: one-line, reversible rule in `judge_node.a1_judge_score()`.

## Evidence (S204, 30/09/2026)
- `llm_call_log` s1_judge, A1 (tenant_id NULL): GPT-4.1 17/09 n=354 judge 8.88 (distinct 8.88,
  fit 8.99); 21/09 n=8 judge 8.50. Luna 30/09 n=10 judge **3.20** (distinct 3.20, fit 7.60).
- `llm_shadow_log` (GPT-4.1 shadow, same requests), 8 A1 pairs: Luna fit 7–8 / distinct 2–6;
  GPT-4.1 fit 9 / distinct 8–9. Luna mission_present True in 7 of 8.
- 3-tour smoke: validate 9.75 → judge 3.0 → HITL on all 3 (grounding never reached).

## Tradeoffs
- Luna brand_fit 7–8 clears MIN_QUALITY 7.0 with little margin; a fit of 6 now retries (with the
  judge's feedback) instead of passing. Kept the 7.0 gate rather than lowering it.
- Switching s1_judge back to GPT-4.1 would also fix A1 but reverses S200's decision for T2 and
  Debate too — rejected.
