## Decisions
- **246 asserts more than `current_user = 'cis_owner'`.** Also `session_user = 'db_migrator'`, schema `shared`
  owned by `cis_owner`, and `db_migrator` a member of `catalog_owner`. Reason: the S22 step is the login switch, not
  the role switch (245 already made the master run `cis/` files as `cis_owner`). With only the `current_user` check,
  applying 246 before the S22 Terraform apply (master login, switched to `cis_owner`) would pass and spend the key
  246 without proving S22. Now it fails there and registers nothing. The `catalog_owner` check proves the recipe's
  precondition, i.e. the end of the XD9 freeze.
- **246 stays assert-only (XD7).** The catalog recipe is exercised in the CI test with a test-only view
  (`catalog.zz246_v_recipe`, created then replaced), not in 246: the plan puts the recipe in "the 246 CI test", and a
  real catalog view in 246 would have to be a contract change of its own.
- **The test builds on `test_aa860_migration_245`'s module fixture** (imported, so the RDS-like master, 244 and 245
  set-up and teardown are not duplicated) and adds rl-002's memberships of `db_migrator` plus a CI-only password.
  `_apply_as_migrator` reproduces DB Migrate's switch (`SET LOCAL ROLE cis_owner` right after the file's `BEGIN;`).
- **No real catalog view is touched in CI:** `CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW` without `WITH (security_barrier = true)` would
  reset the option on `catalog.v_tour` for every later module; the test view carries the option and is dropped.

## Tradeoffs
- Importing the 245 test module ties the two modules: a change to the 245 fixture changes this one's set-up. Accepted:
  246 only makes sense on top of exactly that state, and duplicating ~60 lines of fixture would drift.
- The `session_user` check makes 246 depend on the login name `db_migrator`; renaming the migrator role later needs
  no change here (246 is applied once and never re-run).
