# AA-1077 — T9 blog length contract

Lane l5 of S277. Source: AA-741 phase 3 report (root `docs/audits/2026-11-10-S276-aa741-phase3/`, §4.5 and §9.5).
28 phase 2 blog pieces: 20 held, 13 of them on F4 and all 13 too SHORT (median 652 words); the writer asks for
900–1,300 words but 25 of 28 came in under 900; the short side was repaired by a full rewrite (F4 fixed on 6 of 18,
a new hold gate on 9 of 18, about 1.02 USD); the post-loop normalizer shortened text after F4 had measured it, so 3
of the 7 approved pieces are stored under the floor.

## Decisions

- **One owner: `services/acp_content_writing/length_contract.py`.** It holds the F4 blog band
  (`BLOG_FLOOR_CHARS` 4,700 / `BLOG_CEILING_CHARS` 13,000, tag-stripped), the measured `CHARS_PER_WORD` (5.8), the
  extend target and the side helpers. `quality_gates._BLOG_MIN_CHARS/_BLOG_MAX_CHARS` now read it (names kept for
  their importers: length trim, the blog truth-cut refusal).
- **The writer's word target stays the literal in `prompts.py`.** `_BLOG_WORDS` / `_BLOG_WORDS_MAX` are hashed
  symbols of the registered t9_write prompt (v2/v3): changing their source text needs a prompt version bump plus a
  migration that registers it, for a prompt that renders byte for byte the same. `length_contract.writer_words()` reads
  them (lazy import), so the number is still written once. A test pins the relationship: writer floor × 5.8 ≥ F4 floor
  × 1.08 and writer max × 5.8 ≤ ceiling. Only comments in `prompts.py` changed (the "tags included" claim was wrong);
  `scripts/prompt_registry_check.py` reports 0 problems.
- **F4 codes.** The gate name stays `F4_extreme_length` (taxonomy, route table, writing memory and old ledgers all key
  on it). The blog violation now starts with its side and states words and target:
  `length_short: content is only 3947 chars (about 652 words), under the blog floor of 4,700 chars (about 810 words);
  the writer aims for 900-1,300 words` / `length_long: content is N chars (about W words) — far beyond the blog
  ceiling …`. "only N chars" and "far beyond" are kept on purpose: the audit scripts' regexes and
  `service._F4_OVER_RE` keep working. The other 7 channels keep their wording byte for byte.
- **Hold reason codes.** `gate_eval.hold_reason_codes` gives `length_short` / `length_long` for F4, read from the
  violation (`length_contract.code_for`), so an old ledger re-read gets the split code as well. `length` stays the code
  of an F4 failure whose side does not read and is what labels stored before this change carry: readers group with
  `gate_eval.reason_family()` / `LENGTH_CODES` (backward-compatible reading in views and reports).
- **Re-check after the normalizer** (`service._recheck_length`, the last step of `_normalize_final_text`, blog only):
  F4 runs on the text that is stored. A fresh failure replaces the ledger's F4 entry and holds an APPROVED piece
  (`held_reason` "F4_extreme_length: length_short: …"; its stop label then reads `length_short`). A fresh pass never
  turns a held piece approved (the hold was decided on the gated text). Every blog gets `writer_input.length_trace`
  (admin-only; not a repair-log entry, so `repair_log[-1]` stays the last repair or label): chars after each normalizer
  step (`loop`, `stock_cut`, `authenticity_cut`, `faq_answer_fit`,
  `final_integrity`), the floor, the words and the verdict — the report asked to measure which step cuts.
- **FAQ answer fit is floor-aware** (normalizer only): a drop that would take a blog that was at or over the floor
  under it is not made; the record says `floor_kept`, the admin flag says why. A non-answer FAQ pair is a warning; a
  blog under the floor is a hold. The stock-line cut was already floor-aware (it re-runs F4 and refuses).
- **Extend repair** (`length_extend.py` + `generate.extend_sections`). Attempt 2 of a blog whose F4 failure is the
  short side, with ≥ 1 body section and ≥ 1 seed fact sentence the post does not tell yet
  (`quality_gates.unused_source_facts`, as AA-788's F14 floor rewrite). Up to 3 thinnest body sections (never the
  opening, never the FAQ) get ONE new paragraph each, sized from the deficit to `extend_target_chars()` = floor + 520
  (the largest normalizer cut seen on the phase 2 holds was 557; 5,220 chars ≈ the writer's own 900-word floor).
  Passing sections are never sent to the model. Each paragraph is checked in code: plain prose (no heading, list,
  question, tag, CTA), at most 2× the words asked, `repair_contract.new_specifics` (no number / time / ordinal /
  position / pace word the source lacks — the source-fidelity rule), and a clean seam (`sentence_repair._integrity`).
  Then the attempt runs its deterministic fixes and every T10 gate on the whole text; the AA-528 guard keeps attempt 1
  when the added text broke a passing gate (truth before length).
- **Routing.** The F4-short entry is set aside (`set_aside: length_extend`) and the other failures route as before:
  quoted findings → the sentence repair, then the extend in the same attempt (`repair_mode`
  `sentences+length_extend`); a gate that needs the full rewrite (CTA, F2 lexicon, …) → the full rewrite as before and
  no extend; no plan (no section / no untold fact) → the full rewrite as before. A sentence-repair failure still falls
  back to the full rewrite. An extend call error keeps the text (the gates then hold on length), never a second call.
- **Stage.** The call runs on `t9_write` (the writer, Sonnet 5.5 today), like the attempt-2 sentence repair, with
  `quality_signal.mode = length_extend`, `content_piece_id` set, `record_call_sync(..., **billed_fields(resp))`.
  Registered in `docs/llm/prompt-register.md` as `t9_write.extend` (version `git`, like the other generate.py
  prompts); no manifest bump, so no migration.
- **Default off.** `T9_LENGTH_EXTEND=on` turns it on (worker env, or one audit-tenant job through
  `quality.trial_flags`, now in `ALLOWED["t9_write"]`); unset = main's full rewrite, byte for byte. Reason: the lesson
  "a change of the writing loop that has not had its review rounds ships behind a flag whose default is main" (S272).
  The issue's own order is: trial on 3 pieces, then the 9 length-only pieces, then switch. The switch is classified in
  `writing_memory.config_fp.MODE_FLAGS["t9_blog"]`.
- **Scripts.** `scripts/aa1077/remeasure_length.py` ($0, read-only session): per piece stored chars / words, the
  ledger's F4 reading, chars before (length trace, else the F4 reading) / after the normalizer, the contract's
  verdict and the status the re-check gives; summary `approved_under_floor_stored` / `_after` (must be empty),
  `held_by_recheck`, `length_only` (the trial's input). `scripts/aa1077/extend_trial.py` (paid, `CAP_USD` hard cap,
  read-only session for reads): base gates on the stored text ($0), the extend exactly as the loop runs it, gates
  after with a live F9 judge (`F9=stored` for $0), "would be approved" and new hold gates; it never writes a piece.
  The trial runs on stage `t9_write` (not `adhoc_aa1077`) so it measures the production route; its rows are found by
  `content_piece_id` + `quality_signal->>'mode' = 'length_extend'` in the run window.

## Tradeoffs

- **Hold vs ship an approved piece the normalizer shortened.** Holding is honest (the floor is the contract) and a
  held piece still reaches the tenant (AA-770). The FAQ-fit floor rule removes the most likely cause, so fewer pieces
  should hit the re-check.
- **Append-only extension vs section rewrite.** Appending one paragraph per section cannot break the existing text
  and is checkable sentence by sentence; a section rewrite reads more naturally but brings back the rewrite risks.
- **One call for up to 3 sections vs a call per section.** One call is cheaper and keeps the voice consistent; a bad
  paragraph is dropped alone.
- **Extend target 5,220 chars, not the writer's 900–1,300 words.** The aim is to clear the floor with the normalizer's
  margin, not to pad to the writer's target.

## Decision for the tech lead

The floor question: 12 of the 27 phase 2 seeds were ≤ 991 words and 9 of those 12 pieces were held on F4 (1
approved); of the 15 seeds ≥ 1,210 words, 4 were held on F4 and 6 approved. Length follows the seed (r = 0.424), not
tour days (0.098).

| Option | What changes | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Floor by seed size | F4 floor drops for a thin seed (e.g. 3,500 chars under ~1,000 seed words) | No held piece for a thin seed | Thin articles ship; two floors to explain; SEO value of a 600-word guide is low |
| B. Floor by brand style | The brand pack declares short / standard / long; F4 reads it | The agency decides; fits a short-sentence brand (trail: 5 of 5 holds on F4) | A new brand field + UI; every brand can pick "short"; does not fix a thin seed |
| C. T7 minimum seed for Blog | The T7 Blog Bar also needs a minimum seed (words and untold fact sentences) before a Blog Subject is ready | Fixes the cause; no T8/T9 spend on a piece likely held; one floor stays | Fewer Blog Subjects on thin tours; a T7 change |

**Recommendation: C, with the single 4,700 floor kept and the extend repair as the fix inside T9.** The extend repair
can only add what the seed states, so a seed without enough untold facts cannot honestly reach the floor; that is a
T7 decision, not a T10 one. Set the minimum from the trial: if the extend rescues ≥ 7 of the 9 length-only pieces, a
low bar (≈ 600 seed words with ≥ 3 fact sentences) is enough; if not, ≈ 1,000 words. B stays a later option for the
target and the ceiling only, never below the floor.
