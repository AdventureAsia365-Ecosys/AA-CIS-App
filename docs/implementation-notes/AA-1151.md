# AA-1151 — Supply content: the two-person publish rule (CIS part of the add-on offers slice)

The Booking add-on offers (optional activities per place, Booking bk-068) take their customer text from the CIS supply
content (`/admin/supply-content`). The issue asks for "content drafts in CIS with a second-person publish".

## Decisions

- **Reuse the tour content gate**, not a new rule: `api/routers/admin_pipeline/_publish.enforce_publish_gate` with the
  switch `cis.content.publish_two_person` (CIS 458, Platform › Settings, admin only, maker_checker). One switch for
  every publish of customer text; the tech lead turns it on for both at once.
- **Author of a version** = `created_by` of a `human` version, mapped from the supply actor (`staff:<id>`) to the gate
  actor (`admin:<id>`). An `llm` draft has no human author: the person who reviews and publishes it is the second
  person after the writer.
- **No migration**: no new column or setting; a DB CHECK on `reviewed_by <> created_by` would refuse existing
  self-published rows and the switch-off mode.

## Changed

- `services/supply_content/review.py`: `editor_key`, `version_editors`.
- `api/routers/admin_supply_content.py`: the publish route reads the switch, then (only when on) the author and
  runs the gate.

## Tradeoffs

- With the switch off (Dev default) nothing changes: one person may still write and publish. That is the tech lead's
  S285 D1 decision for tour content, applied the same way here.

## Should know

- The gate needs the caller's identity (`x-admin-user-id` / the verified staff identity); a secret session without a
  person (`admin`) is never a same-person author.
