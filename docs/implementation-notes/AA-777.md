# AA-777 — F9 judge consistency: sticky pass, attempt-3 truth cut on blogs, judge A/B

Branch `feat/aa777-f9-sticky-pass-cut` (S239). Scope 1 + 2 in code; scope 3 (judge A/B, also
feeds AA-714) is an adhoc script the orchestrator runs in the api container
(`AA-Ecosys/.tmp-session/s239/aa777_judge_ab.py`), plus a $0 shadow readout for AA-714
(`AA-Ecosys/.tmp-session/s239/aa714_shadow_readout.py`).

## Decisions
- **Sticky pass lives in `gate_brand_voice`**, fed by a per-job `set[str]` that `run_write_background`
  creates once and hands to every attempt through `run_quality_gates(f9_passed_sentences=...)`. The
  gate mutates the set (adds what a verdict passed), the caller owns it. `None` (every other caller,
  admin re-check, scripts) = no memory, the old behaviour.
- **"Passed" = a body sentence (prose / bullet / FAQ answer, at least 4 words, the
  `locate_sentence` floor) that the verdict did not flag in ANY way**: not an unsupported claim
  (justified or not), not a broken sentence, not quoted in the notes, and containing no flagged
  phrase. Each passed sentence is stored as a key (`_f9_key`): the sentence AND the one before it,
  tag-free, case/accent-folded, symbols and punctuation kept. A repaired sentence, a changed neighbour
  (a repaired referent: 'We visit X. It was founded in 1692.') or a new symbol ('12+' for '12') is a
  new key and is judged again. (Review on cc265e22: first cut keyed on the `_cmp_fold` sentence alone,
  which also dropped symbols.)
- Only **unsupported claims** are dropped when sticky. TEXT_INTEGRITY and the CTA check are not:
  a broken sentence or a missing CTA is not the judge-noise pattern S234 measured (10/15 reverted
  pieces re-flagged a claim sentence passed one attempt earlier).
- A reasonless verdict (`F9_NO_REASON`) and a judge outage add nothing to the set (no real verdict).
- **A verdict adds nothing either when** (review on cc265e22, both reproduced as tests):
  - a claim or broken-sentence quote locates in no body sentence (a paraphrase, or `_fuzzy_locate`
    ambiguous between two) and is not the search title / meta: the verdict flagged a sentence nobody
    can name, and that sentence would otherwise go into the set and have its exact re-flag dropped;
  - the reply was salvaged by json_repair (`parse_judge_json_checked` reports it) or stopped at
    `length` / `max_tokens`: the entries after the cut would read as passed. The salvaged flags still
    count. Logged `F9_sticky_pass_skipped`.
  - A note quote that locates nowhere does not void the verdict (notes often quote the SOURCE back);
    it only keeps out every body sentence it copies (`hygiene.copies`, either way).
- A sticky sentence quoted in the judge's notes is never turned into a blocking TEXT_INTEGRITY item
  by the notes fallback (review on cc265e22).
- A judge reply that is a JSON list / scalar is now "judge unavailable" (was an AttributeError outside
  the gate's try).
- A claim found only in the search title / meta is never sticky (it does not locate in the body).
- **Blog truth cut runs BEFORE the gates of attempt 3, instead of the second repair call**, not after
  them as the LinkedIn `_cut_truth_items` does. The gates (F9 included) then re-judge the cut text, so
  no waiver logic is needed: a clean cut ships approved; anything left holds as usual. It covers F1
  (`F1_grounding`) too, which the LinkedIn cut does not.
- "Still failing after one sentence repair" is checked at gate level: an earlier repair_log entry with
  `repair_mode == "sentences"` whose own trimmed ledger (AA-791) had F1 or F9 failing and blocking
  (`_truth_repaired_before`). After a full rewrite, or a style-only repair, the truth item still gets
  its one repair.
- **Refusal rule** (`_blog_cut_refusal`): no cut when `delete_flagged` refuses (hook, CTA, FAQ question,
  orphaned pronoun, integrity), when the cut text is under `_BLOG_MIN_CHARS` (measured on the
  whitespace-normalised tag-free text, a little stricter than F4's raw measure), or when the cut breaks
  a FAQ contract / complete ending that held before. A refused cut falls back to the old path (one more
  repair of every item); the hold stays if that fails.
- The S234 r3 attempt-3 fallback (repair call fails → `delete_flagged`) obeys the same refusal rule on
  blogs; before, it could cut a blog under its minimum.
- Removed (instead of finishing) the half-done blog branch inside `_cut_truth_items`: one mechanism for
  the blog, and `_cut_truth_items` stays LinkedIn-only as tested (`test_other_channels_unchanged`).

## Changed
- `quality_gates.py`: `_f9_flagged_folds`, `_f9_passed_folds`, `_drop_sticky_claims`;
  `gate_brand_voice(passed_sentences=...)`; log `F9_sticky_pass`; `sticky_pass` (sentences) on the
  F9_cta_fact entry and `sticky_pass` (count) in the t10_judge call log extra;
  `run_quality_gates(f9_passed_sentences=...)`.
- `sentence_repair.plan`: each item carries `gate` (which failed gate it came from).
- `service.py`: `BLOG_TRUTH_CUT_ATTEMPT`, `TRUTH_CUT_GATES`, `_truth_repaired_before`,
  `_blog_cut_refusal`, `_cut_blog_truth_items`; loop wiring (repair modes `truth_cut`,
  `truth_cut+sentences`, `truth_cut+sentences_cut`, refusal reason in `truth_cut_refused`); logs
  `t9_blog_truth_cut` / `t9_blog_truth_cut_refused`; the job's `f9_passed` set.
- AA-786 review leftover ("tag-before-punctuation counting, fix before AA-777"): cut counting now uses
  `_cut_key` (no space before a mark) on both sides, in `_cut_truth_items` and the blog cut. Before, a
  kept sentence whose tag sat before its full stop ('Punakha [R:a1].') was counted as cut — on a
  partial LinkedIn cut that waived F9 while the flagged hook was still in the post (test added).
- CONTEXT.md: the "Blogs keep the plain hold until AA-777" line replaced by the S239 behaviour.

## Tradeoffs
- Re-judging after the blog cut costs one F9 call (~$0.003) but keeps a single, honest verdict path;
  the alternative (cut after the gates + waiver) would ship a blog whose cut text was never judged.
- Keying on (previous sentence, sentence) instead of the sentence alone: a sentence after a repaired
  one loses its sticky status even when its meaning did not depend on it. Accepted: one extra honest
  judgement is cheaper than shipping a claim whose referent changed. Not cleared after a full rewrite:
  a pair reused word for word in a rewrite keeps its local context.
- Voiding the whole verdict on one unlocatable claim quote loses that attempt's memory; preferred over
  guessing which sentence a paraphrase meant.
- Gate-level "repaired before" is coarser than per-sentence tracking (a repaired sentence is new text,
  so per-sentence identity does not survive a repair anyway).

# r6 follow-up — repair guard, plan merge, sticky key, thin-coda frame, F17 cut (S239)

Branch `feat/aa777-repair-no-new-specifics`. Input: the r6 readout (17/20 approved; retry-added
blocking fail 4/26 = 15.4% vs target <= 8%; AA-768 fixed set 3/5 vs >= 4/5) and the diagnosis
`AA-Ecosys/docs/audits/2026-10-16-S239-in-progress-audit/aa777-r6/diagnosis.md` (fixes ranked 1-8).
Implemented: fixes 1, 2, 3, 4, 6. Not implemented: 5 (pronoun-follower cut), 7 (F16 second FAQ
regeneration), 8 (readout violation-level count — readout script, not CIS code).

## Decisions
- **Fix 1 — no new specifics in a repair.** `sentence_repair.splice_checked(..., trip_ctx, source)` first
  drops every replacement that ADDS (vs the sentence it replaces) a number the source lacks (the F1
  reading, `find_novel_numeric_claims(year_forms=True)` against `source` + the original sentence), a
  time / ordinal / position the seed does not give, or on a blog a pace / rest word the source does not
  back (`trip_facts.new_specific_claims`, the F17 functions themselves). That item keeps its old
  sentence (`guard: "specifics"`, findings in `specifics`); the job logs `specifics_reverts` and
  `specifics_problems` on the repair_log entry. Plus one line in `_REPAIR_SYSTEM`.
  - Revert, not cut: a cut at attempt 2 can take a blog under its minimum and `splice_checked` does
    not know the channel. A reverted F1/F9 item stays failing and goes to attempt 3 (blog: the truth
    cut; LinkedIn: the last-attempt cut-not-hold) — never a NEW failure.
  - Time phrases are compared by their time word ('the afternoon' / 'in the afternoon' →
    'afternoon'), so a rewording of a time the sentence already had is not new, and R1 ('arrive at the
    afternoon', exempt from `_TIME_RE` by the S234 carve-out) → 'the afternoon starts' IS new.
  - "Services" (a new meal, guide, transfer) are not checked: no deterministic test without false
    positives; the F9 judge stays the check for those.
- **Fix 2 — `plan()` merges a second finding on the same sentence** instead of dropping it: union span,
  problems joined with '; ALSO: ', `gates` lists every gate, `gate` prefers F1/F9 (so the attempt-3
  truth cut sees it). A family-caveat ('add ONE') item is never merged (first finding wins, as before),
  and a merge whose wider span would swallow one is skipped.
- **Fix 3 — sticky key.** `_f9_key` carries the previous sentence only when the sentence opens on a
  referent word (`_REFERENT_OPEN_RE`: it/they/this/that/these/those/here/such/which…). A judge quote
  over several sentences is split (`_f9_quote_keys`) and is sticky only when every sentence is.
  `_f9_located_key` stays for one-sentence lookups (returns None for a multi-sentence quote).
- **Fix 4 — thin coda.** `_trim_thin` first tries the old trailing-appositive head (so 'On Day 14, …'
  keeps its day), and only then drops a leading frame ('Before / After / On leaving / On the way …,'; r7 review: sequence frames only)
  and tries the rest whole, then its heads. The F17 thin-coda message names the extra words
  ('words the source does not give — leaving, part, northern, mongolia'), keeping 'cut the added
  clause' and no `: '` before the quote (plan() still parses it).
- **Fix 6 — the blog attempt-3 cut takes F17 'cut it' items** (problem matches `cut it` / `cut the added
  clause`; never 'add ONE', never the hazard 'keep the hazard, cut the reassurance') once F17 went
  through a sentence repair before (`_cut_gates_repaired`). Truth items keep the #761 rule (any F1/F9
  repaired before). `_cut_blog_truth_items(repaired=...)` defaults to the old F1/F9-only scope; the
  entry gains `cut_gates`. Same refusal rule (`_blog_cut_refusal`: blog minimum, FAQ, ending, hook,
  pronoun orphan).
- **F16 not changed.** The second FAQ regeneration (fix 7) is not "clear": the `flags` column of
  1a8dec5d / 3532ecdd (F7_faq_autofix: "FAQ regeneration not kept (…)" vs "dropped FAQ …") was not
  dumped, and a regeneration at attempt 2 writes new FAQ text the F9 judge then reads — a possible new
  retry-added F9 fail, the metric this round is trying to lower. Read the flags first ($0).

## Changed
- `sentence_repair.py`: `new_specifics`, `_drop_new_specifics`, `splice_checked(trip_ctx=, source=)`;
  `plan()` → `_merge_item`, `_span_text`, `TRUTH_GATES`; items carry `gates`.
- `trip_facts.py`: `unsupported_time_keys`, `_pace_hits` (pace_claims now built on it, same output),
  `unsupported_pace_words`, `new_specific_claims`; `_LEADING_FRAME_RE`, `_thin_head`, `_trim_thin`
  frame pass; thin-coda message lists the extra words.
- `quality_gates.py`: `_REFERENT_OPEN_RE`, `_f9_key` (conditional previous sentence), `_f9_quote_keys`,
  `_f9_located_key` on top of it, `_drop_sticky_claims` uses every sentence of a quote.
- `service.py`: `F17_CUT_GATE`, `_F17_CUT_RE`, `_cut_gates_repaired`, `_cuttable`;
  `_truth_repaired_before` now a wrapper; `_cut_blog_truth_items(repaired=)` + `cut_gates`; loop passes
  `trip_ctx` + `grounding_text` to `splice_checked`, logs `specifics_reverts` / `specifics_problems`.
- `generate.py`: one sentence in `_REPAIR_SYSTEM` (no new time / day part / ordinal / pace word).
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_aa777_r6_fixes.py` (28; the r6 sentences replayed — R1, R2, H2 pace,

## Tradeoffs
- The Tradeoff above ("keying on (previous sentence, sentence)") is narrowed: a non-referent sentence
  now stays sticky after its neighbour is repaired. Accepted per the #761 design (a sentence the judge
  passed, unchanged, is not re-litigated); a referent-led sentence still re-judges. Risk: a sentence
  whose meaning depends on its neighbour without a pronoun ('The monastery was founded in 1692.' after
  'We visit X.' changed to 'We visit Y.') stays sticky — rare, and F1 still checks its numbers.
- Merged items show the repair two problems in one PROBLEM line; a two-sentence span gets one
  replacement string. Cheaper and simpler than ordering two edits on overlapping spans.
- Reverting (not cutting) a repair with new specifics can leave a truth item failing one more attempt.

# r6 follow-up — review fixes (S239, two reviews of 37513ae7)

## Decisions
- **Hazard never cut (review D1 / defect 1).** `sentence_repair.keeps_sentence(problem)` ('add ONE' or 'keep the
  hazard'). `service._cuttable` returns False for it before the truth branch; `delete_flagged` skips it;
  `splice_checked` does not splice an empty replacement of a hazard item (`guard: "hazard_kept"`, logged as
  `hazard_kept`); `splice`'s repeat guard keeps a hazard sentence (`repeat_kept`) instead of deleting it.
  - Departure from review 1's "don't merge": a hazard finding DOES merge (either order). Not merging drops
    the second finding, and in the real ledger order (F1, F17 timing / pace, then the hazard, then F9) the
    hazard is often second — dropping it left a cuttable item on the hazard sentence (probe: timing +
    hazard → `_cuttable` True). Merged, the keep mark stays in the problem, so the item is never cut and
    the repair is shown both findings. The family caveat ('add ONE') is still never merged.
- **Multi-sentence cut (review D2).** `delete_flagged` checks every sentence span inside [start, end) for
  hook / CTA / FAQ question / heading, and runs `_safe_to_delete` on the last one (so the sentence after
  the item is the one tested for an orphaned pronoun).
- **No widening into the hook (review 2 defect 2).** `_merge_item` clamps the union so it never starts in
  the first prose sentence when `hit` did not already hold it (F1 on S2 + F9 quote 'S1 S2' → item stays S2;
  the attempt-3 cut works again).
- **Sticky key openers (review D3 / defect 3).** `_REFERENT_OPEN_RE` adds an optional conjunction
  (and / but / yet / so / still / or), there / then / afterwards / later / nearby / inside / beyond /
  from there / from here / after that / after this, and any 'the …' opener (definite noun phrase).
- **Thin-coda frame (review D4).** `_LEADING_FRAME_RE` keeps only sequence frames (before / after / on
  leaving / on the way); a conditional frame (when / once / while / as) is never dropped.

## Changed
- `sentence_repair.py`: `keeps_sentence`, `_keeps_hazard`, `_hook_span`; `_merge_item` (hook clamp, docstring),
  `splice` (hazard repeat guard), `splice_checked` (hazard_kept), `delete_flagged` (all spans of the item).
- `service.py`: `_cuttable` (keep mark first; the 'add ONE' clause folded into it); `hazard_kept` log key.
- `quality_gates.py`: `_REFERENT_OPEN_RE`. `trip_facts.py`: `_LEADING_FRAME_RE`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_aa777_r7_review_fixes.py` (28; 22 fail on 37513ae7), incl. the two gaps from

  family-caveat repair in source words ('before lunch') that is not reverted.

## Tradeoffs
- 'The …' as a referent opener is broad: a sentence opening on 'The' re-judges when the sentence before it
  changed. Costs one more judging of that sentence (a possible retry-added F9 re-flag); missing it lets a
  claim the repaired neighbour no longer backs ship unjudged. Only the sentence right after a repaired one
  is affected; r6 17125e0c ('After a short trek …') stays sticky.
- A merged hazard item that also carries a false F1/F9 claim is never cut at attempt 3: if the repair
  fails twice the piece is held (admin-only), never shipped without its hazard.
