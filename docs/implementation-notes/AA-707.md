# AA-707 — T2 keywords for the tenant's market

## Decisions
- `s1_prefetch.tenant_market_seo()` resolves the tenant's buyer market (same resolver as research)
  and reads `acp_contract.search_demand` for that market: keywords naming the tour's strong title
  places (or its activity), volume > 0, top 10 + their PAA. Zero DFS purchases.
- T2 uses it when found; otherwise falls back to the admin `seo_context` row (US) — now with its
  PAA too — and logs `t2_market_seo_fallback`.

## Should know
- Coverage depends on Segment research having run for that market and those places; a market with
  no research for the tour falls back to the admin row.
