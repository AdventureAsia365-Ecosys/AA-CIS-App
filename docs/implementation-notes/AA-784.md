# AA-784 — Place cards for the whole catalogue: drafter route

## S252 (26/10/2026) — draft on GPT-6 Luna, check stays on Haiku 4.5 (migration 286)

Input: the S252 place-card model evaluation in the root repo, `docs/audits/2026-10-26-S252-place-card-model-eval/`
(`README.md`, decision rule `design.md` §7, fixed before the paid run). 30 hard place groups over 13 countries, every
fact labelled against web sources by a blind panel, decisive labels re-checked adversarially; run cost $1.839.

| Pair (draft × check) | Wrong kept facts (FK) | Invented facts | Wrong identity | Useful facts / card | All 9,186 cards | §7 gates |

| GPT-5.6 Luna × Haiku 4.5 (live) | 5/125 = 4.0% (run 2: 2.4%, LK 4.1%) | 7/132 = 5.3% | 0 | 3.73 | ≈ $16 | fails 3 (FK) / 4 (LK) |

Four pairs passed every gate and tie on FK, useful facts and identity; the cost tie-breaker picks GPT-6 Luna × Haiku 4.5.

### Decisions

- **Migration 286** (`api/migrations/286_place_card_draft_gpt6_luna.sql`, byte copy in AA-CIS-Infra
  `db/migrations/cis/`): `a3_place_card` → role `validate`, provider `openai`, model `gpt-6-luna`, `acc3`. Same shape
  as 222 (unconditional `UPDATE ... WHERE stage = 'a3_place_card'`): the eval decision applies whatever the row holds.
  `a3_place_card_check` is not touched (Haiku, fallback `{sonnet}`).
- **Fallback `{gpt-5.6-luna}`, not 222's `{sonnet}`.** The checker is Claude Haiku. With a Claude draft fallback, every
  card drafted while the primary fails is drafted and checked by the same vendor (222 accepted that). GPT-5.6 Luna is
  another vendor than the check and is the previous live drafter, measured in the same eval (FK 2.4–4.0%), so a
  fallback card is no worse than today's. The check's own Sonnet fallback stays Anthropic against an OpenAI draft, so
  every combination of the two chains is cross-vendor (unit test pins it). No OpenAI-platform key, no shadow.
- **Catalog guard + enable.** 170 seeded `gpt-6-luna` disabled ("Bedrock agreement not accepted on acc3 yet"). Dev
  already routes four stages on it and the eval called it, so a still-disabled row (a fresh DB) is enabled by 286 with
  a NOTICE. A missing row, a non-`converse` row, no `acc3` profile or no price refuses the file. A disabled / missing
  fallback `gpt-5.6-luna` is only a NOTICE (the route skips it).
- **`DRAFT_MAX_TOKENS` 1500 → 4000.** GPT-6 Luna reasons inside `max_tokens`: at 1500 it returned an empty reply in
  the S246 trial. The eval ran it at 4000: 30 drafts, max 1,384 / p90 1,201 output tokens, all `end_turn`; the
  orchestrator's read of `llm_call_log` history gives p90 1,956. Billing is per token used.
- **Check stays at 1024**, now named `CHECK_MAX_TOKENS`: Haiku 4.5 ran at 1024 in the eval (177 checks, max 490,
  all `end_turn`).
- `SAFE_DEFAULTS["a3_place_card"]` mirrors 286 (`gpt-6-luna`, fallback `gpt-5.6-luna`).

### Tradeoffs

- **Catalog-unreadable behaviour.** Both draft keys are catalog keys, so with the catalog unreadable the draft call
  raises (no card, no miss stored; the next build tries again) where 222's legacy Sonnet would still have drafted.
  Accepted: card builds are jobs, a missing card only means fewer background facts, and the alternative breaks the
  vendor rule exactly when things go wrong.
- **Coverage.** 88% of identifiable groups by the rule's wording; 79% if the 4 ambiguous groups counted (then the
  winner would be GPT-5.6 Luna × Opus 5.5 at ≈ $72). GPT-6 Luna declines vague names instead of guessing.
- **Slightly fewer facts** (U 3.44 vs 3.73), still above the 2.5 floor.
- **Checker recall is weak** (Haiku 4.5: 8/34 natural wrong facts, 22/23 planted), so quality rests on the drafter;
  that is why the drafter changes and the checker does not.

### Should know

- **Deploy order: app image (4000 tokens) BEFORE DB Migrate 286.** On the old image a T8 request could draft on GPT-6
  Luna at 1500 and store a paid `no_name` miss on an empty reply.
- Existing cards are not redrafted by 286; the AA-784 B build runs per country after Nghiệp's OK (3 → ≤ 50 → per
  country, cap ≈ $17 = $13 × 1.3), cost per batch from `llm_call_log`.
- Rollback is in 286's header (the 222 row); leave `gpt-6-luna` enabled, other stages route on it.
- Integration test not run locally (the local `cistest` role is not SUPERUSER, so the replay stops at the 279 grant
  for both 282's and 286's tests); 286's scenarios were checked by hand on the local DB in rolled-back transactions.

## S253 (27/10/2026) — spot-check fixes: region / locality rules, unverifiable-fact filter, card rebuild

Input: the blind 20-card spot check of the live build (GPT-6 Luna × Haiku 4.5), root repo
`docs/audits/2026-10-26-S252-place-card-model-eval/spot-check/README.md`: FK 3/83 = 3.6% (gate ≤ 3%), 7/20 wrong
locality / region, C07 identity unclear. Fix the system, not the 11 cards.

### Decisions

- **No migration, no route change.** The prompts live in code (`_CARD_SYSTEM` / `_CHECK_SYSTEM`); `PROMPT_SHA` rotates
  by itself, so stored misses are retried once when a build next meets them (bounded: one tour / one atom per
  call). `a3_place_card` (GPT-6 Luna) × `a3_place_card_check` (Haiku 4.5) stay as migration 286 left them.
  `RULE_VERSION` is unchanged (the free skip rule did not change).
- **Region rule** (`region_problem`): first-level administrative division or empty. Cleared when it is the card's
  country (by name / ISO name only — `shared.countries` aliases include sub-national names such as Tibet), the place
  itself (exact name, or the card's own name ends in Province / Prefecture / Aimag / State …), a trekking or
  marketing area (a short list: Khumbu, Everest, Annapurna, Langtang … Golden Triangle, Gobi; or ending in
  Trek / Circuit / Trail / Route), a landform area (Valley, Delta, Plateau, Highlands, Desert, Coast …) without an
  administrative word, an Atoll / Islands / Hills area only with a direction word ("North Malé Atoll" goes, "Kaafu
  Atoll" stays), or a direction word + a country name ("Northern Mongolia", "Central Vietnam", "South India";
  Inner Mongolia / South and Western Australia / Northern Ireland are allow-listed divisions). A region carrying an
  administrative word (Province, State, Autonomous, District …) is never cleared by the shape rules: "Central
  Province", "Rift Valley Province", "Tibet Autonomous Region" stay; so do "South Gyeongsang", "West Bengal",
  "Central Visayas". A comma list ("Ladakh, Gilgit-Baltistan, Xinjiang" for a range) is left alone.
- **Locality rule** (`locality_problem`): the settlement the place is IN, or empty. Cleared when it is the place
  itself (a town's card), repeats the region word for word (the region stays), the place's HEAD word is remote —
  last word camp / hut / pass / glacier / peak / summit / mountain / volcano / lake / tso / valley / gorge /
  desert …, a leading Lake / Mount, a park phrase at the end ("Kilimanjaro National Park"), a last word "La"
  (Tibetan pass) — checked on the display name and on every itinerary name of the card's atoms, or the place is a
  village ("village" in a name; "-gaon" / "-gaun" only on a Nepal card) and the locality is another settlement.
  A building named after a park ("Sagarmatha National Park Visitor Centre" in Namche Bazaar) is not remote. A
  generic name ("City Palace") keeps its locality: its card key needs it.
- **Clear, never guess.** The rules only empty a field; they never write a value. An empty locality / region is not an
  error (the spot check scored null as right for remote places); a wrong one is.
- **Rules run before the check**, so Haiku checks the identity that will be stored. The check now returns
  `locality_ok` / `region_ok`; an explicit false clears that field instead of failing `identity_ok` (which now
  covers display name + country). A missing key keeps the field (older reply shape).
- **Unverifiable facts** (`unverifiable_claim`): present-tense status ("currently", "is still", "continues to",
  "serves / functions / operates as", "today/now … houses / part of"), administrative role ("is … the capital /
  seat of / administrative centre", with no past marker such as was / former / imperial / royal in between, and not
  "in the capital") and authorship ("designed / planned / engineered by", "architect", "the work of") are never kept,
  whatever the check says. Exception: a national capital — "capital [and <≤2 words> city|town] of (the) <card
  country's name or ISO name>" with no administrative word (province, state, provincial, region …) elsewhere in the
  sentence ("Malé is the capital and most populous city of the Maldives", "Hanoi is the capital of Viet Nam"); not
  an alias ("Lhasa is the capital of Tibet" on a China card is dropped). Review fix S253: the first version kept any
  sentence naming the country within 8 words of "capital", which let "Pleiku is the capital of Gia Lai Province in
  Vietnam" through. The check prompt asks for "unsure"
  on the same claims; the drafter prompt asks not to write them.
- **Seed-time filter for cards built before the rule.** `atom_seed.writer_facts` applies `unverifiable_claim` to
  every card a T9 writer sees, unless an admin verified the card. This fixes the existing ~4.7k cards for writers
  at $0 without re-drafting them (same pattern as AA-774's kind filter). The admin view still shows every fact.
- **Seed-time identity rules for cards built before them (review fix S253).** `atom_seed.writer_identity` runs
  `clean_identity` on a copy of a non-verified card (place_names = the atom's itinerary name) before the
  `Location:` line: the writer no longer reads "Barafu Camp, Moshi" or "Northern Mongolia" from the ~4.7k stored
  cards. Free, only ever clears a field; the stored card is unchanged and the admin view shows it as stored.
- **Rebuild entry**: no job kind took card ids, so `place_cards_build` gets an optional `{"card_ids": [...]}`
  payload (no tour_id) → `place_cards.rebuild_place_cards`: re-draft + re-check each card IN PLACE under the new
  prompts and rules (≤ 40, one draft + one check each). The card keeps card_id, place_key, display_name, country,
  links and aliases — the checked draft is held to the stored name and country, so stored = checked; only
  locality, region, naming_note and facts change. A draft placing it in another country changes nothing
  (`country_changed`); no_name / identity_rejected leave the card as it was (the spent cost is added to the card,
  no place_card_miss row: the place has a card). Verified / rejected cards and generic-name cards are skipped.
  When the new check keeps no fact, the old facts minus the unverifiable ones stay (a rebuild never empties a card
  that had checked facts). Admin entry `POST /admin/content-quality/place-cards/rebuild` (QUALITY_WRITE, uuid
  validation, `max_attempts=1`: a retry would pay again for cards already rebuilt).
- **C07 (identity unclear)** is not rebuilt: it is held with status `rejected` (the existing admin status — writers
  never see a rejected card) plus an audit row, until an admin decides which crossing the tours mean. No new
  "needs review" status: that would need a migration and every `status <> 'rejected'` reader to change.
  Review fix S253: C07 is stored under the compound country "Nepal and China" (pre-AA-990), so a new draft (one ISO
  country, another place_key, never `_same_identity`) would have been INSERTed as a fresh auto_checked card and
  linked with no review. `find_rejected_for_draft` now blocks a draft that names (core / feature-synonym key) a
  REJECTED card whose stored country is a compound naming the draft's country: no insert, no link,
  `identity_rejected` miss. Scope kept to compound countries: a rejected SINGLE-country card under another (legacy)
  key keeps the AA-772 rule (its atoms get a fresh card — `test_atoms_linked_to_a_rejected_card_are_resolved_again`),
  and one under the same key the `_store_card` rule.
- **Ops script** (root `.tmp-session/S253/aa784_rebuild.py`, ECS-exec style, not committed): MODE=dry | apply |
  hold_c07 | report. C-id → card_id from the spot-check `packet.json` ("card" → "card_id").

### Tradeoffs

- **Measured on the spot-check packet (83 facts, 20 cards) and the eval's 30 reference identities:** the fact filter
  drops C02 f0, C18 f1, C20 f2 (the 3 contradicted facts) and 1 supported fact (C11 "The town of Bulgan is the
  provincial capital" — true, but exactly the claim class that went stale for Pleiku): FK on the packet 3/83 → 0/79,
  1/80 supported facts lost. Identity rules fix 5 of the 7 bad cards deterministically (C04 both fields, C11, C13,
  C14, C15) and introduce no wrong value; they also clear 4 correct-but-redundant or nearest-valley values (C02
  locality Pleiku = the place, C14 locality Malé = the place, C03 Shigar and C19 Marsyangdi on village names).
  On the 30 reference identities they clear only redundant or remote localities and one geographic region
  ("Central Cambodia").
- **C08 (Goa Velha, a neighbouring village) and C17 (wrong side of a county line) are not detectable without a
  gazetteer.** They rely on the prompt + the check's `locality_ok` / `region_ok` + the rebuild.
- **Word lists are short and explicit** (lessons: prefer structure over word lists). The structural parts carry most of
  the weight (head word of the name, direction + country, admin word present); the trekking-area list covers the
  names the drafter is known to use as regions in AA's catalogue. A first-level-division gazetteer per country would
  be stricter but is not in the repo.
- **Remote head words lose some correct localities** (an urban lake such as Hoan Kiem Lake loses "Hanoi" as locality,
  an "Elephant Camp" its town). The region stays, and an empty locality is never wrong.
- **National capital rule is strict on purpose**: a true national-capital fact phrased with an administrative word
  ("Prague is the capital of the Czech Republic" on a Czechia card — the card's name/ISO name is "Czechia") or by an
  alias is dropped. Dropping a true fact costs one line; keeping a stale provincial seat is the defect.
- **Compound-country hold, not all rejected cards**: a rejected single-country card under a legacy key can still be
  re-created under the new key by a draft repeating its identity (pre-existing AA-772 behaviour, unchanged).
- **Seed-time filter changes T9 seeds** for cards with such facts (fewer facts; a card dropping under 3 facts may
  bring sibling lines back, AA-772 rule). Accepted: these are the facts the spot check found false.
