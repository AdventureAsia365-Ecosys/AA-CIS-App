# AA-1081 — T7 Blog topic coverage: a no-topic reason per adopted tour, at least one Blog topic per tour

Lane l20 of S277 (Claude Code, orchestrated). Source: root audit `docs/audits/2026-11-10-S276-aa741-phase3/` §4.3.2,
§4.3.3, §9.3 (6 of 48 tour × tenant pairs had no Blog Subject: 4 below the Bar, 2 with free candidates and no
proposal; the agency was not told why). Builds on AA-1079 (Debate rank only by default, `T7_DEBATE_MODE`) and the
AA-1077 memo (option C: a T7 minimum seed for Blog).

## Decisions

- **Where the reason lives: the `t7_slate` job result, not a table.** `propose_slate()` already returns its result into
  `shared.job.result`, and `GET /v1/slate` already reads the last successful proposal for `unmatched_markets`. The
  job result gets `blog_coverage` (per adopted tour: topics, reason code, admin counts); `GET /v1/slate` returns a
  tenant view of it (`blog_topics`: tour id, catalog tour name, topic count, reason code; nothing else).
  No migration, no CIS number taken.
- **Typed reason codes** (`slate.NO_TOPIC_REASONS`), checked in this order for a tour with no Blog topic:
  1. `not_ready` — no ranked moment of the tour in the tenant's markets yet;
  2. `low_search_interest` — no moment clears the Blog demand / question bar (caf4bf16 in the audit);
  3. `no_place_match` — moments clear it, but none has a seed atom on the tenant's tours (AA-857 rule);
  4. `seed_too_thin` — every free moment is thin (S271 walk rule) or under the Blog minimum seed (below, when on);
  5. `no_fitting_topic` — every free moment was dropped by Debate (cut mode only; never named as a judge to the tenant);
  6. `places_covered` — the moments' places already have a Blog topic or article (picked / used / dismissed, or another
     tour's topic at the same place).
  The tenant copy (Partner web) says what it means for the agency, never "bar", "Debate", score or gate.
- **The guarantee: a coverage pass before the round robin** (`_choose_slate(cover=…)`, Channels with
  `cover_each_tour` in `CHANNEL_BARS` = Blog). For each Channel it walks the Channel's queue (Bar-cleared, Debate-
  flagged last, rank order) and takes the first candidate that covers a tour still without a topic, under the same
  rules as every seat (held place, thin / short seed, one place per Channel, cross-Channel cap, hub cap). A candidate
  covering several uncovered tours covers all of them (greedy, in rank order, deterministic). The round robin then
  runs as before. Tours that already have a picked / used Blog Subject are covered (a dismissed one is not).
  Reserving first, rather than adding a seat after the round robin, keeps the cross-Channel place cap intact: the
  coverage seat counts against it before the social Channels take the place.
- **Minimum seed for Blog behind `T7_BLOG_MIN_SEED_WORDS`** (env, integer words; unset, 0 or not a number = off, the
  default). When on, a Bar-cleared, non-held, non-thin Blog candidate whose Blog seed is under the minimum is not
  proposed on Blog (it stays on every other Channel's queue: "given to a shorter channel"); the tour's reason becomes
  `seed_too_thin` when that leaves it without a topic. The tech lead's AA-1077 decision only sets the number (the
  memo: ≈ 600 words, or ≈ 1,000). An env flag like `T7_DEBATE_MODE`, read per call.
- **What "Blog seed words" means** (`services/acp_shared/blog_seed.py`): the audit's measure (`o1 PIECE SEED`: the
  writer's seed words + its route moment words). Estimated at T7 from what the pick would give T9: the seed atom's
  `atom_seed_text` plus `route_moment_seeds` over the walk `_segment_walk` resolves (the pick's own rule), excluded
  moments dropped as `_fetch_route_segments` does. Same word regex as the audit script.
- **Segment → tours**: `_SEGMENT_CANDIDATES_SQL` returns `tour_ids` (the tenant's tours with a current, non-excluded
  ranking row of the Segment in the tenant's markets); a Candidate carries them. The coverage counts a topic for
  every tour its Segment is on (the audit's "covers 3 of your tours").

## Changed

- `services/acp_shared/slate.py`: `Candidate.tour_ids`; `tour_ids` in `_SEGMENT_CANDIDATES_SQL`;
  `_fetch_segment_candidates(unseeded_out=)`; `CHANNEL_BARS[*]["cover_each_tour"]`; `_choose_slate(cover=)` with a
  shared admit rule; `_fetch_short_blog_seeds()`; `blog_tour_coverage()` (pure), `tenant_blog_topics()` (pure);
  `_HELD_BLOG_TOURS_SQL`, `_TOUR_NAMES_SQL`; `propose_slate()` result `blog_coverage`.
- `services/acp_shared/blog_seed.py` (new): word count, the setting, the T7 estimate of the Blog seed.
- `api/routers/v1_planning.py`: `GET /v1/slate` returns `blog_topics` (null before the first proposal with coverage).
- `scripts/quality/replay_aa1081_blog_coverage.py` (new, $0, read-only): today's candidate pools of every tenant (or
  the AA-741 audit tenants) through the production slate code, cover off vs cover on, per tour: topics and reason.

## Tradeoffs

- **No incremental slate in this slice.** The issue also asks for an incremental slate (only new or changed Segments)
  and before / after run time. Not built here: it changes when `propose_slate` re-reads and what it deletes, and the
  Debate cache (AA-1079) already keeps the paid part flat. Left as a follow-up with the run-time numbers from the
  replay as the baseline.
- **Demand-join check for low-demand tours** (caf4bf16: place terms 201,240 US vs best Segment 1,300) is not changed
  in code: the replay prints, per `low_search_interest` tour, the best Segment demand and questions so the join can be
  checked; changing the join is an A3 ranking change, not T7.
- **Coverage reads are advisory.** If the tour list / names / held-tour reads fail, the Slate is proposed as before
  (no coverage pass) and the result says `blog_coverage: {"status": "unavailable"}` (log `slate_blog_coverage_failed`).
- **The minimum-seed estimate is not free when on**: per Blog-cleared free candidate it resolves the walk and reads the
  seed (a few dozen small reads). It runs only when the setting is on, inside the background job.

## Should know

- Tenant-facing fields are only `tour_id`, `tour_name`, `topics`, `reason`; the per-tour counts (`detail`) stay in the
  job result (admin). `GET /v1/slate` filters the reason to `NO_TOPIC_REASONS`.
- A topic counted for a tour is a proposed / picked / used Blog Subject whose Segment is on the tour at proposal time.
  A tenant who dismisses (cuts) a topic sees the tour's reason only after the next proposal.
- The Partner web copy for each code lives in `web/app/(studio)/portal/_components/writer.messages.ts` (Partner PR).
- `scripts/` is in `.gitignore`: the replay script is force-added (`git add -f`), like the other `scripts/quality/*`
  files. The unit and integration tests load it by path, so a checkout without it fails them.
- The replay is not in the api image (the Dockerfile copies `api/ shared/ services/ worker/` only). On Dev it runs
  through the S3-mediated ECS exec: `cd /app && PYTHONPATH=/app python3 /tmp/replay.py --slug-prefix audit-`.

# Lane l5 of S278 — T7 slate run time (Debate judge concurrency, cache kept for unchanged Segments)

Live evidence (S278, 4 `t7_slate` jobs on the audit tenants, CIS 318a7982): 756-894 s per job against the S276 p50
of 232 s. Almost all of it is `t7_debate_judge` run one call after the other: 319-326 ok calls per job at ~2.2 s each
(sum 635-766 s), ~0.037 USD per job, plus 25-36 refused shadow calls on acc3. The same tours were judged for these
tenants in S275, yet no call was served from the cache.

## Decisions

- **Two passes in `debate.assess_candidates()`.** Pass 1 walks the candidates in order on the one connection
  (contested, cache read, the evidence read for a miss) exactly as before. Pass 2 runs the misses: the Jev pre-filter,
  then (grey) `score_candidate_fit` in a worker thread, its `record_call(**billed_fields)` row and the cache write
  (`_judge_brand_fit`), at most N at once (`asyncio.Semaphore` + a `ThreadPoolExecutor` of N threads). Each signal is
  stored at its candidate's index, so the signal list (and the rank / cut / Slate built on it) is identical to a
  serial run; the unit test compares serial vs 8-wide on a judge whose replies finish out of order.
- **The setting is an env flag, `T7_DEBATE_CONCURRENCY`** (integer, read per call; unset / not a number / < 1 = 8,
  > 32 = 32; 1 = the old serial run). Same kind of switch as `T7_DEBATE_MODE`; not a key in
  `services/quality/thresholds.yaml`, which holds calibrated score cut-offs under change control, not throughput knobs.
  No task-definition change is needed: 8 is the default the moment the code deploys.
- **The gateway still bounds every call.** Each thread runs with a copy of the caller's context
  (`contextvars.copy_context().run`), so the job's tenant (`call_log.current_tenant_id`) and capacity line reach
  `LLMClient`; the per-connection `max_concurrency` / `rpm_limit` semaphore and the TPM reservation
  (`breaker.limit` / `capacity.reserve`, thread safe) and the tenant AI budget check (`tenant_budget.check`, per call)
  apply exactly as before. A call that would exceed a limit waits there or is refused by the gateway; Debate counts
  a refusal as it always did (the candidate passes, `error`).
- **One connection, one statement at a time.** The cache write goes through `conn` under an `asyncio.Lock`;
  `record_call`, `record_billed_failure` and Jev's `decide()` open their own short connections (as before), so up to
  N extra short connections run at once during the pass.
- **Failure rule unchanged.** A failing judge call (malformed reply, refusal, budget stop) makes that one candidate
  pass with `brand_fit_source="error"` and its billed cost row; the other calls go on.
- **Why the cache missed (finding).** The cache key `(tenant, segment_id, brand_version)` has no model or route
  version in it, and no TTL: neither explains a full miss. The cache is emptied explicitly by A3 (AA-645 step 0,
  T13): `_a3_atomize_chain` dropped the cache rows of **every** Segment of the tour (all tenants, plus the Routes
  through them) after **any** atomize that was not `skipped`. Day fingerprints are keyed per content version
  (`atomize_day_fingerprint.tenant_tour_version_id`), so a new published version of a tour re-reads every day
  (status `success`) even when the itinerary text gives back the very same atoms (atom ids are content hashes of the
  place and action). The S277/S278 recompose of the served Masters (path A-new: a new published version per tour,
  A3 re-atomize follows) and the S277 reruns did exactly that, so every Segment of the audit tenants' tours lost its
  score and the next `t7_slate` re-judged all of them. This is a defect: the rule meant "the atom text changed", the
  code checked "atomize ran".
- **The fix: clear only Segments that changed.** `export.handler.segment_states()` reads, for the tour's Segments,
  the label (`canonical_place`, `canonical_action`: the judge's topic) and every member atom id (any tour: the judge's
  evidence) before and after the segment stage; `changed_segments()` names the Segments whose label or member set
  differs, or that are missing from either read; only those lose their cache rows. The before-read is taken after
  atomize but before the segment stage, which is where the memberships change (new atoms join, the member rows of
  dead atoms are dropped, AA-988), so it also covers atoms an earlier failed attempt of the same job wrote
  (`AtomizeIncomplete` raises before the segment stage). The check now runs on every atomize, also `skipped` (two
  small reads); if a state read fails, every Segment of the tour is cleared, the old behaviour.
- **What still invalidates (correctly):** a brand version change (new key), a Segment whose member atoms changed
  (re-judged on the new evidence), a relabel.

## Changed

- `services/acp_shared/debate.py`: `debate_concurrency()`; `assess_candidates(concurrency=)` in two passes;
  `_judge_brand_fit()` (one cache miss, the LLM call site, registered in
  `tests/unit/test_aa927_llm_call_site_registry.py`); `_brand_fit_signal()`; log line `debate_judged`
  (candidates, judged, concurrency).
- `services/export/handler.py`: `segment_states()`, `changed_segments()`, `_SEGMENT_STATE_SQL`; the atomize chain
  clears only the changed Segments; outcome gains `brand_fit_segments_changed` (and `brand_fit_cache_cleared` is now
  always present, 0 when nothing changed).
- `scripts/quality/bench_aa1081_debate_concurrency.py` (new, $0, force-added): the production `assess_candidates`
  over 320 invented misses with a fake judge.
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_aa1081_debate_speed.py`, `tests/integration/test_aa1081_segment_states_sql.py`; the
  expected atomize outcome in `test_aa526_a3_atomize_trigger.py` and the skipped-atomize test in
  `test_aa645_step0_publish_atomize.py` follow the new keys.

## Tradeoffs

- Width 8 by default: 320 misses at 2.2 s go from ~12 min to ~1.5-2 min. The OpenAI direct connection's own
  `max_concurrency` / TPM rows decide whether 8 really run at once; wider is a single env change, not a deploy.
- Threads, not an async gateway call: `score_candidate_fit` and `LLMClient.generate` are synchronous (the judge client
  is shared with the S1/T2 graphs); moving it off the event loop also stops the job's loop from being blocked for
  ~2 s per call (the old NOTE in the code).
- The cache-invalidation fix keeps the explicit T13 clear instead of adding an evidence hash to the cache key: a hash
  column would be a contract migration (ALTER of an existing table, MIG-4 chain) for the same result.
- A budget stop mid-pass: up to N calls are already in flight when the tenant budget refuses; each still makes its own
  per-call check, so the overshoot is at most N judge calls (~0.0001 USD each).

## Should know

- Benchmark (`PYTHONPATH=. python3 scripts/quality/bench_aa1081_debate_concurrency.py --candidates 320 --widths
  1,4,8,16`, reply time 2.2 s scaled by 0.01, local): width 1 = 737 s projected (the S278 jobs: 756-894 s), 4 = 184 s,
  8 = 98 s, 16 = 51 s; the signals are identical at every width. The projection leaves out the per-call
  `record_call` / Jev connection set-up, which now overlaps too.
- The first `t7_slate` after this deploy still re-judges what the earlier clears emptied (the cache is lazy); the
  second refresh with unchanged Segments should show ~0 `t7_debate_judge` calls. A rerun / recompose that really
  changes atoms still re-judges the changed Segments only.
- Before / after evidence lives in the jobs: `shared.job.result` of `a3_atomize` (`brand_fit_cache_cleared`,
  `brand_fit_segments_changed`) and of `t7_slate` (`segments_seen`), and the `debate_judged` log line of the worker.
