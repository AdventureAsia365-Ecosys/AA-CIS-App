# AA-820: AI acceptable-use summary on the portal `/terms`

Linear AA-820 (Tenant Terms clause for the Anthropic Usage Policy, effective 12/11/2026). The full AI Schedule,
its clause-to-policy map and the questions for counsel live in the private root repo
(`docs/legal/tenant-terms-ai-clause-draft.md`, Draft v0.2 of 24/10/2026). This repo carries only the public summary.

## Decisions

- `/terms` §3 is retitled "3. AI-generated content and acceptable use" and summarises Schedule .2–.6 in plain
  English: follow the model providers' usage policies (links to the Anthropic Usage Policy and the AWS Acceptable
  Use Policy), nine prohibited uses, the partner adds any AI label the law or a channel requires, the partner reviews
  and is the publisher, and Adventure Asia may hold, rewrite or decline content. Unpublishing and suspension point to
  §4 and §5, which already describe them.
- The existing portal AI-notice bullet is kept word for word.
- `LEGAL_VERSION` becomes "Draft v0.2" and `LEGAL_UPDATED` "24 October 2026". Both are shared with `/privacy`; the
  shell states one version for the legal pages, so `/privacy` shows v0.2 too although its text did not change.
- Nested list style: `li ul` gets `circle` bullets and a 6 px top margin so the sub-list reads as part of its bullet.
- New test file `tests/unit/test_aa820_portal_terms_ai.py` instead of growing the AA-886 file, so the AA-886 test
  stays about the shell and routing.

## Changed

- `portal/app/terms/page.tsx` (§3 and the header comment), `portal/app/_legal/LegalPage.tsx` (two constants, one
  CSS rule), `CONTEXT.md` (legal pages line no longer says the clause "waits for AA-820"), the new unit test.
- No other copy changes. The draft banner and `robots: noindex` stay.

## Tradeoffs

- The summary is published while it is still a draft (the banner stays). The alternative, keeping
  "[to be confirmed]" until counsel approves, leaves partners with no stated AI rules before 12/11/2026. Ms. Thư's
  options are in the root readiness packet §12.
- "Telling Adventure Asia to publish a piece ... confirms that you have reviewed it" mirrors Schedule .5(b). It is a
  statement about the partner's duty, not a claim that the system forces a review step (it does not).

## Should know

- Self-check against the product: the summary does not promise per-user publish records (`acp_shared.publish_log`
  has no per-user publisher column), retention numbers or notice periods.
- Portal-only change: Vercel deploys it; `deploy-dev.yml` does not watch `portal/**` or `tests/**`, so no ECS
  restart. It must merge and deploy before the AA-979 P2a-1 portal cut.
- Local checks: the three portal legal/split unit test files (45 passed), flake8 on the new test, eslint on the two
  portal files, a TypeScript syntax transpile. No `next build` locally (RAM); CI and the preview UI smoke are the
  judges.
