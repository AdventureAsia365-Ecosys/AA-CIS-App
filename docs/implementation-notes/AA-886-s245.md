# AA-886 (S245) — draft /privacy and /terms on the tenant portal

Part A of the issue only (pages + links). The legal packet (part B) stays open.

## Decisions

- **/privacy becomes a portal page.** AA-885 redirected it (307) to adventure.asia/privacy-policy. That
  policy is the company-wide one and does not describe portal data (API key sign-in, brand settings,
  generated content, channel connections, AI processing, activity records). The redirect is removed; the
  portal notice links the company policy as the wider document. Both pages now answer 200.
- **Draft is enforced by the shell.** `portal/app/_legal/LegalPage.tsx` (portal-owned, not a vendored kit
  file) always renders the "Draft — pending legal review" banner. `tests/unit/test_aa886_portal_legal_pages.py`
  checks that both pages use the shell, are noindex, stay outside the `proxy.ts` gate, are not redirected,
  and are linked from the sign-in form and the sidebar.
- **Light theme pinned** like `/tenant-login` and the old `/terms` page (public pages, no theme toggle).
- **Links in ink + underline, not gold.** `BRAND.accentDeep` (#9E6E16) is 4.47:1 on white and 4.05:1 on
  the gold tint, below WCAG AA for body text. The banner keeps the gold tint and border.
- **Open legal points stay visible** as `[to be confirmed]` (controller entity, legal basis, transfer
  safeguards, retention of publish/audit records, DPA with partners, content rights, fees, SLA, liability,
  governing law, AI acceptable-use clause from AA-820).

## Tradeoffs

- Inline styles + one scoped `<style>` block (paragraph/list rhythm) instead of Tailwind classes: the
  portal's public pages (`/tenant-login`, old `/terms`) are styled inline with the brand tokens, and the
  Tailwind 4 preflight removes list bullets, so the shell restores them explicitly.
- No shared component with TripPlanner: different repos, audiences and data.
