# AA-1190 — My work slice 1 (CIS part)

Slice 1 of the S295 My work proposal: every staff role lands on My work, the missing approval tiles, zero tiles
collapsed. Read-only, no migration. The Booking part is in AA-Booking `docs/implementation-notes/AA-1190.md`.

## Decisions

- **Landing.** `landingPage` sends admin to `/admin/my-work` like the content roles (S295 decision D1). A custom role
  with `ops.read` lands there too (it used to land on Overview); the route opens to `ops.read` so such a role gets an
  empty page with the menu, never a 403. The logo goes to `landingPage(role)` instead of a fixed `/admin/overview`.
  The mobile bottom bar shows My work first; Overview only for a role that may open it but not My work.
- **Bands, not the v2 contract.** Each tile now carries `group` (`approve`, `work`, `platform`, `watch`) and
  `counts_toward_total`. The full `my_work.v2` shape (scope, preview, focus, age limits) stays for slice 3.
- **Approval tiles count what the viewer may decide.** Settings proposals and compliance exceptions leave the
  viewer's own proposals out only while two-person approval is on (the approve routes refuse them then); while it is
  off the administrator may self-approve (recorded), so they count. The viewer identity is `admin:<id>` of the
  verified company sign-in, the same identity the approve routes compare.
- **Settings tile link.** Platform › Settings shows one app tab at a time, so the tile opens the tab of the oldest
  proposal with `?pending=1` (new: only the settings with an open proposal, and a "Show every setting" button). Only
  the CIS and TripPlanner tabs list proposals from `shared.setting_value`; Booking and CRM proposals live in their own
  apps and are not counted here.
- **Erasure tile** needs `tenant.write` **and** the administrator role: the approve route checks the role, so a custom
  role with `tenant.write` would see a tile it cannot act on.
- **Platform tile** (`ops.write`): open `acp_shared.ops_event` problems (alarm, service_down, rollout_failed; sev1-3)
  from the status sampler, one cheap read on the partial index, instead of building the whole status verdict (which
  calls CloudWatch, the other apps and Redis). Hidden for the business owner, who reads the status page and does not
  acknowledge alerts.
- **Review Queue split.** `review_waiting` now counts `policy=none` (new filter value: without the elephant-riding
  policy reviews) and `review_held_by_policy` counts `policy=elephant_riding` in the watch band. The Review Queue page
  reads `?policy=` from the link and offers "Without a policy hold".
- **Place cards** move to the watch band and leave the total (backlog).

## Changed

- `api/routers/admin_my_work.py`: Viewer, bands, 5 new tiles, counters take `(conn, viewer)`, a counter may return its
  own `filter`.
- Count helpers next to each list: `settings_registry.open_proposals_summary` (shares `OPEN_WHERE` with the page
  list), `compliance_exceptions.proposed_summary`, `tenant_erasure.store.awaiting_approval_summary`,
  `ops_status.store.open_problems_summary`, `review_queue_summary(policy=)`.
- POLICY `GET /admin/my-work/summary` adds settings.approve, compliance.write, tenant.write, ops.write, ops.read
  (snapshot regenerated). The built-in roles that reach it are unchanged (admin, content_lead, content, tenant_qa).
- Frontend: `lib/myWork.ts` (bands, `splitBands`, total without watch tiles), the page (bands, the empty queues folded
  into "Nothing waiting in N queues"), `lib/rbac.ts` (landing, `MY_WORK_CAPS`), `lib/reviewPolicy.ts` (new,
  dependency-free), Review Queue and Settings read the new link filters.

## Tradeoffs

- The settings tile counts proposals; the page in pending mode lists settings and shows the proposal count in a badge.
  A key with a platform and a tenant proposal is one row and two proposals.
- When proposals exist on both the CIS and TripPlanner tabs the tile total covers both but opens the oldest one's tab.
- The platform tile is not the status page's "Needs attention" count (that one also includes SLO burn, cost, stale
  sources). It says so in its info text.
- No age limits yet (slice 3 adds `age_limit_days` as settings); this slice adds no threshold.

## Should know

- The proposal's "Ops: things need you" maps to the platform tile here; acknowledging stays on Overview.
